# How CapacityLens compares

CapacityLens answers one question: who is free next week? The table lists what other
common tools do, so you can pick the one that fits how your agency plans.

| Tool | Granularity | What it tracks | Hosting | Price model | Pick it when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spreadsheet | Whatever you lay out | Whatever you type | A file you share | No licence fee | You have a handful of people and one person keeps the sheet up to date. |
| Float | Hours or percentages | Scheduling, capacity, and time tracking on its higher plan | Hosted by the vendor | Per scheduled person, per month | You want hour-level scheduling or time tracking in the same tool. |
| Forecast (Harvest) | Days and hours on project timelines | Assignments, workload, and scheduled against actual time through Harvest | Hosted by the vendor | Paid subscription; see the vendor's pricing | You already track time and budgets in Harvest and want planning beside it. |
| Resource Guru | Hours per booking | Bookings of people and other resources, including repeat and multi-resource bookings | Hosted by the vendor | Per person, per month | You book rooms, equipment or vehicles as well as people. |
| CapacityLens | Weeks on screen; bookings are date ranges with a daily amount | Who is booked, free or over capacity; time off; placeholders for people you have not hired | You host it, or use the demo in your browser | Free software under AGPL-3.0; you pay for your own server | You want a week-by-week capacity view with no budgets, timesheets or task tracking, and your data on your own server. |

Competitor facts checked 2026-10-01.

Vendors change their plans. Check the vendor's own pages before you decide.

## What CapacityLens leaves out

CapacityLens does not track budgets, timesheets or tasks, and it does not schedule by the
hour. See [Philosophy](/reference/philosophy) for why. If you need any of those, one of the
tools above is the better choice.
