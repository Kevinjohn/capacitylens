# Set up your company as Owner

The Owner's shortest setup route is to create the company, appoint an Admin, and confirm
that they have joined. You can then stop. The Admin can prepare the schedule without
requiring you to add sample people or work first.

## Prerequisites

- The technical installer has given you the CapacityLens address and first-Owner sign-in route.
- You know who will handle everyday company administration.

## 1. Claim the Owner account and create the company

Follow [Create your company](/owner/create-your-company). Confirm the fixed week start,
timezone, and language choices with your team before saving them.

## 2. Prepare the schedule

Skip this stage when another person will be the Admin. If you will administer the company
yourself, follow [Admin and settings](/admin/) after creating the company.

## 3. Invite an Admin

Follow [Appoint an Admin](/owner/appoint-an-admin). Send the one-time link privately and
wait until the person appears in the members list with the Admin role.

## 4. Compare members with scheduled people

The Admin can create scheduled people and optionally associate them with members. A
member can sign in; a scheduled person has capacity to plan. Creating one never silently
creates the other.

## 5. Hand over routine administration

Send the new Admin the [Admin and settings guide](/admin/). They can invite teammates,
add scheduled people, choose settings, prepare work, and make the first booking.

## 6. Transfer ownership when responsibility changes

Ownership transfer is not part of initial handover. When it is needed, the Owner
nominates an active Admin, that Admin agrees, and the same Owner confirms. See [Roles and
permissions](/getting-started/roles-and-permissions#hand-the-company-to-someone-else).

## What's next

Read [Owner responsibilities](/owner/responsibilities), or stop until an Owner-only
decision is needed.
