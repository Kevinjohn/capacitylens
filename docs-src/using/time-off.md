# Time off

![Placeholder: Time off showing company closures and personal time off entries](https://placehold.co/686x385?text=Time+off%3A+company+closures+and+your+dated+entries)

Find your name under Personal time off to check recorded absences.

Company closures appear separately above it. The page shows current and upcoming entries.

## On Schedule

![Placeholder: Schedule showing the same personal absence beside planned work](https://placehold.co/686x385?text=Schedule%3A+the+same+personal+absence+beside+work)

The same absence appears as a hatched block beside your planned work.

Time-off notes are visible only to Owners and Admins.
