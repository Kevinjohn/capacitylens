# Overview

![Overview ledger showing each person's free days out of capacity for this week and the next three weeks](../screenshots/flows/using_overview_1.png)

Overview shows each person's free days out of their capacity for this week and the next three
weeks. The bar under each figure is green, amber or red as free time falls, with tentative work as
a grey hatch.

Select Has availability to show people with spare capacity. Select Totals to add each week's
committed percentage and free days above the table.

## Look further ahead

![Overview with twelve week columns, weekly totals and the Load curve display](../screenshots/flows/using_overview_2.png)

Choose 8 weeks or 12 weeks to add more complete weeks. Choose Load curve to compare the shape of
each person's free time without the figures.

The avatar beside a name opens that person's [work list](/using/read-the-schedule#your-work-list).

If Overview is missing from your menu, your company may restrict access.

Overview does not show capacity figures in Blocks mode.
