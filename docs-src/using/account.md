# Account

![Placeholder: Account showing the menu location, signed-in identity and account controls](https://placehold.co/686x385?text=Account%3A+menu+location+identity+and+sign-in+controls)

Account is near the bottom of the left menu, alongside Sign out and Switch company.

It shows your signed-in identity and available security controls. These differ between password sign-in and company login.
