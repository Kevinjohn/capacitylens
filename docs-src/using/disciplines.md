# Disciplines

Create the groups, such as Design or Development, that organise people on Schedule and give their
rows a shared colour.

![Disciplines showing group names and colour swatches](../screenshots/flows/using_disciplines_1.png)

Create a discipline here, then assign it to people in [Resources](/using/resources#add-a-person).

## Add or change a discipline

![Add discipline with the preset colour swatches open](../screenshots/flows/discipline_colour_form.jpg)

Select **Add discipline**, enter a name, choose a preset colour and save. It appears on Schedule
once someone is assigned to it, at the end of the schedule's group order. This list stays
alphabetical.

![Edit discipline with its existing name and colour](../screenshots/flows/discipline_edit_form.jpg)

Select the edit icon on a row to change its name or colour. A blank name keeps the form open until
you correct it.

## Remove a discipline

![Delete discipline confirmation explaining that resources are ungrouped rather than deleted](../screenshots/flows/discipline_delete_confirmation.jpg)

Select the delete icon on its row and confirm **Delete discipline?** Its people are ungrouped,
not deleted, and keep their bookings. They appear in the **Unassigned** group, or the Studio and
Supplementary bands, until you assign another discipline.

Removed the wrong one? **Undo** restores the discipline and reconnects people who are still
ungrouped. Other edits to those people remain. **Redo** removes the group again.

## Turn disciplines off

When the company turns **Use disciplines** off in [Settings](/using/settings#disciplines), the
**Disciplines** page, its menu link and the Resources form's **Discipline** field are hidden;
`/disciplines` sends you to Schedule. Nothing is deleted, and turning it back on restores the
groups.
