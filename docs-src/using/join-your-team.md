# Join your team

<span id="steps"></span>

Accept your agency's invitation or company joining link and open its schedule. An invitation names
the company and your proposed role. A joining link names the company and applies its joining
policy.

## Accept an invitation

![Invitation preview showing company, role, expiry and the intended email hint](../screenshots/flows/join_invitation_preview.jpg)

Open the link and check the company, role, expiry and any email hint. Opening it only previews the
invitation. If it is tied to an email address, only that address can accept it.

- With a password sign-in, enter that email and password, then select **Accept invite**.
- If company login is offered, choose **Sign in with Google**, complete sign-in and return to
  accept. A new Google identity can continue only when the invitation is addressed to its verified
  Google email; returning from Google does not itself accept the invitation.
- If you're already signed in, check the identity shown is the invited address. If not, select
  **Use a different account** before accepting.

![Create account tab on an invitation with Name, Email, Password and Create account and accept](../screenshots/flows/join_invitation_create_account.jpg)

New here? If **Create account** is offered, select it, enter your name, the invited email and a
password, then select **Create account and accept**. That one step creates your sign-in and
accepts the invitation.

You land on the company's Schedule with the invited role. If you were already a member, you keep
your existing role; ask an Owner or Admin to change it.

## If the invitation doesn't work

If the invitation is for the wrong email, has expired or was already used, ask an Owner or Admin
for a new one.

## Join with a company link

![Reusable company joining page asking for an existing proven identity](../screenshots/flows/join_company_link_entry.jpg)

1. Check the page names the company you expect.
2. Enter your email, then choose **Sign in with Google** or sign in with an existing password
   account. A new Google identity needs an unused invitation addressed to its verified email;
   company joining policy cannot create an identity.
3. Select **Join company**.

A Google callback creates or signs in to an identity, but does not add company membership or use an
invitation. Finish by accepting the invitation or selecting **Join company**. The server checks the
current company policy and email proof at that point. A joining link can't create a password
account. Open-registration and approved-domain policies need proof that you own your email. People
who join by policy get the **Viewer** role; an addressed invitation can give the role it names.

## If the page can't prove your email

- Select **Email me a verification link**, if shown, and open the message while signed in. Select
  it again if nothing arrives.
- If you do not have an existing Google identity, ask an Owner or Admin for an invitation addressed
  to your verified Google email.
- If Google sign-in is unavailable, ask the installer to check the company's sign-in settings.

If joining is refused, ask an Owner or Admin whether the company's policy allows your address or
whether your access is disabled.

## Check your access after joining

![Team & access showing the role and its restrictions after entering a company](../screenshots/flows/viewer_access_current.jpg)

Your role is under **Team & access → Your access**. Joining doesn't put you on the schedule.
If you can't find your row, ask your Admin to add you in **Resources** and link it to your sign-in.
