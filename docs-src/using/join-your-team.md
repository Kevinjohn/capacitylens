# Join your team

<span id="steps"></span>

Accept your agency's emailed invitation and open its schedule. The invitation names the company
and your role.

## Accept an invitation

![Accept invite page showing the company, the invited role, Name, Password and Accept invitation](../screenshots/flows/join_invitation_preview.jpg)

1. Open the link from the email and check the company and role.
2. Enter your **Name** and choose a **Password**, then select **Accept invitation**. Your sign-in
   uses the address the invitation was sent to.
3. Select **Ask for access**, then wait for an Owner or Admin to approve you.
4. Select **Check again** when they have. The company's Schedule opens with the invited role.

## If the invitation doesn't work

If the link has expired or was already used, ask an Owner or Admin to select **Resend**. If the page
says your address already has a sign-in, sign in instead and ask them or the installer to record the
invitation for your existing account. If your access was declined, ask them whether that was a
mistake.

## Check your access after joining

![Team & access showing the role and its restrictions after entering a company](../screenshots/flows/viewer_access_current.jpg)

Your role is under **Team & access → Your access**. Joining doesn't put you on the schedule.
If you can't find your row, ask your Admin to add you in **Resources** and link it to your sign-in.
