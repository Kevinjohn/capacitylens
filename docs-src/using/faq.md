# Day-to-day FAQ

These answers cover the questions people most often have while using the schedule.

## Why can I see a booking but not change it?

Your role may be Viewer. Viewers can read the schedule and find capacity but cannot change
company data. Ask an Owner or Admin if your responsibilities now require Editor access.

## Why is an empty day not available?

The person may not work that weekday, may have time off, or the company may be closed.
CapacityLens calculates capacity from working days as well as visible bookings.

## Why did the utilisation percentage change when I changed Weeks visible?

Schedule utilisation uses the dates currently visible. A two-week view and an eight-week
view answer different questions, so they can show different percentages for the same person.

## Why can I not find Overview?

Companies can limit Overview to Owners and Admins, or extend it to Editors or everyone.
Use Schedule when your role does not have Overview access.

## Can I use CapacityLens without a connection?

Turn on **Make this device available offline** in your Settings to keep a seven-day,
read-only snapshot on that device. You cannot queue changes while offline. See [Offline
access](/guide/offline-access).

## Where do clients, projects and activities come from?

An Admin or Owner normally prepares them. If your role allows it, the [Quick
start](/getting-started/quick-start) shows the basic order.
