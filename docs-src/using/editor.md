# Plan work as an Editor

![Schedule showing existing bookings, time off, utilisation and the controls for adding work](../screenshots/flows/editor_schedule_current.jpg)

As an Editor you keep the team's plan up to date: bookings, resources, time off, clients,
projects, activities and ordinary company settings. Admins and Owners manage membership. Only the
Owner controls joining policy, imports and company deletion.
[See your work](/using/) covers joining and finding your way round the Schedule.

## Join the right company

![Team & access identifying the Editor role and its restrictions](../screenshots/flows/editor_access_current.jpg)

Open **Team & access** near the bottom of the menu and check your role. If it says Viewer, ask an
Owner or Admin for Editor access. If you belong to several companies, use **Switch company** to
choose the plan you mean to change; your role can differ in each.

Signing in alone does not make you a member: follow [Join your team](/using/join-your-team) with
the address you were invited on. Being on the schedule does not give someone a sign-in, either.

## Check the period and the person

Before booking, read the person's row as a whole: utilisation, working days, time off and existing
bookings. A blank cell does not prove spare capacity. Clear any filter when expected work is
missing.

The percentage beside each person covers only the dates on screen. It turns red when the person is
over capacity in the next 14 days, whatever you are viewing. At 100% a person is fully booked; over
capacity means allocated time is greater than available time.

If **Overview** is in your menu, use **Has availability** to compare the team week by week. Only
Owners and Admins see it by default; an Admin can open it to Editors.
[Read the schedule](/using/read-the-schedule) and [Overview](/using/overview) explain the figures.

## Prepare what the booking needs

Check **Resources** for the person and their working pattern. An invitation creates a sign-in
membership, not a schedule row, so add the resource first if the person is missing. When your
company uses disciplines, **Disciplines** holds their groups.

Check **Clients**, **Projects** and **Activities** for the work. A project activity belongs to its
project; an internal activity needs no client; an All-projects activity can be booked against any
project. Use existing records rather than making a second copy. If your company allows it, you can
also add an activity from the booking form. [Resources](/using/resources),
[Disciplines](/using/disciplines), [Clients and projects](/using/projects) and
[Activities](/using/activities) explain the forms, privacy, placeholders and deletion.

## Make and check a booking

![Editor allocation form with dates, daily hours, status, note, working-day exception and capacity warning](../screenshots/flows/editor_allocation_form.jpg)

1. On **Schedule**, select **+** beside the person's name, or drag across the dates on their row.
2. Choose the project and activity, then the dates. Enter the amount in your company's units:
   Hours, Days or Blocks.
3. Mark the booking tentative or add a note if that helps. If you use **Repeat**, check the preview
   before saving.
4. Select **Save**, then check the bar sits on the right row and dates, and look at utilisation.

Bookings use only the person's working days and the company's working week, within the person's
start and end dates. Time off and closures reduce availability. **Ignore working days** lets a
booking span non-working days, but a new booking must still start on a working day inside the
person's dates.

An over-capacity warning is for you to resolve; CapacityLens never moves work for you. Blocks
show bookings without measuring capacity. [Schedule and change work](/using/schedule-work) covers
every field, repeats and gesture.

## Keep the plan current

Select a bar to edit its amount, dates, status, note or assignee, or to duplicate or delete it.
Drag a bar to move or reassign it, or drag an edge to change its dates; the same working-day rules
apply.

**Undo** and **Redo** reverse changes still in your undo history. Check the schedule afterwards: a
teammate's later edit is not undone.

Record absences and closures on **Time off**, then check the affected weeks. Time-off notes are
visible only to Owners and Admins, even though you can create the entry. [Time off](/using/time-off)
covers repeats, conflicts and closures.

## Choose your display and protect your sign-in

**Settings → My display** changes this browser only. **Company setup** and **Scheduling features**
affect everyone, so check the plan after changing them. Week start and time zone are fixed when the
company is created. [Settings](/using/settings) explains each setting.

**Account**, at the bottom of the menu, shows your sign-in, its security controls and **Sign out**.
Your sign-in can belong to several companies, so a password reset or session revocation by an
Admin applies to all of them. Ask an Owner or Admin if you are locked out.
[Account](/using/account) has the details.

## When something interrupts the work

### A booking cannot start on the selected date

Check the person's start and end dates, their working pattern and the company's working days, then
start on an allowed working day. Read the form's error first: **Ignore working days** does not
remove every date restriction.

### A save failed or another person changed the same record

Use the retry or recovery control offered. If CapacityLens replaces your edit with the current
saved record, review it before editing again. A bar you can still see is not proof its last change
was saved. [Day-to-day FAQ](/using/faq) covers connection messages.

### I can see a code name but not a client's real name

Only the Owner sees confidential real names, whatever your role. Use the code name.

### I am using an offline snapshot

The snapshot is read-only, even for an Editor, and expires after seven days. Edits are never queued
for later. Reconnect before changing work. See [Offline access](/using/settings#offline-access).
