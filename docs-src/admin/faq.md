# Admin FAQ

These answers cover the questions Admins most often have while maintaining a company.

## Why did inviting someone not put them on the schedule?

A member is someone who can sign in. A person is a schedulable resource. Create both when
a teammate needs access and their time needs planning. You can optionally link the two
records in **Team & access**.

## Can a scheduled person exist without a sign-in?

Yes. Freelancers and other people can appear on the schedule without becoming members.

## Which role should I choose?

Use Viewer for read-only access, Editor for planning changes, and Admin for member and
company administration. There is one Owner, who retains the most sensitive decisions.

## Can I retrieve an invitation link later?

No. CapacityLens displays the one-time link once and stores only a hash. Revoke the
pending invitation and create another if the link is lost.

## Can I change the week start, timezone, or language?

No. Those shared calendar rules are fixed when the company is created. Other scheduling
defaults remain available in Settings.

## Do I configure company login?

You prepare member emails, communicate the change, and check that people can get in. The
service operator configures provider credentials and deployment settings; send them to
the [Self-hosted operations guide](/operations/).
