# Administer your company

![Team & access: Invite someone dialog](../screenshots/flows/admin_invite_teammates_1.png)

This guide is for the Admin who prepares a company for everyday use. You do not need the Owner
or installation guides: invite your teammates, add the people you plan for, choose the company
settings, prepare clients and work, and make the first booking. An Owner can do the same work,
and an Editor can change most planning settings, but member access is yours and the Owner's.

A member and a scheduled person are separate records. An invitation gives someone sign-in access
with a role; adding them in Resources gives them a row and capacity on the schedule. Some
teammates need both, and Link to Resource connects the two without changing access. After setup
you own member access, working patterns, closures and company settings; ownership transfer,
import and company deletion stay with the Owner, and backups, upgrades and company login stay
with the service operator.
