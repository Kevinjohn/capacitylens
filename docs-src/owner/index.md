# Set up your company as the Owner

This guide is for the first Owner after CapacityLens has been installed. Your shortest
successful route is to create the company, appoint an Admin, confirm they have joined,
and hand everyday setup to them.

## Your route

1. [Create your company](/owner/create-your-company) and choose its shared calendar rules.
2. [Appoint an Admin](/owner/appoint-an-admin) with a single-use invitation.
3. Confirm the Admin has joined. You can stop here and send them the [Admin and settings
   guide](/admin/).
4. Read [Owner responsibilities](/owner/responsibilities) for the small number of actions
   that only you can perform.

If you plan to administer the company yourself, continue into the [Admin and settings
guide](/admin/) after creating the company.

## When you are done

The company exists, an Admin can complete everyday setup, and you know which decisions
still require the Owner.
