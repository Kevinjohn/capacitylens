## Finding your way around

<span id="faq-access-find-sections"></span>

### Why can’t I see a section or open a page?

The left menu shows the sections available to your access. The current section is marked in the menu. You can also press **⌘K** on macOS or **Ctrl+K** on Windows or Linux to search for a page, person, project or date, then choose an available result. If a page address no longer works, return to **Schedule** from the not-found page. Ask your Owner or Admin if you think a section should be available to you.

<span id="faq-access-navigation-size"></span>

### Can I make more room for the schedule or use the menu on a narrow screen?

On a desktop, select **Collapse menu** in the sidebar header, or press **⌘B** on macOS or **Ctrl+B** on Windows or Linux. Select **Expand menu** to restore it. On a narrow screen, open the menu to see the available sections; choosing one closes the menu. The schedule supports viewing on a narrow screen, but mobile scheduling workflows are not provided.

<span id="faq-access-guidance"></span>

### Where can I get a tour or find help?

The first time you enter, CapacityLens may show a short introduction. Reopen it with **How CapacityLens works** in the sidebar. Owners, Admins and Editors see **Getting started**, with steps for preparing a useful schedule. Owners and Admins can dismiss it for the whole company. Editors can show or hide the panel, but cannot dismiss it for everyone. Viewers use **Help → Show tour** instead. For a tour suited to your role, open **Help** from the left menu and select **Show tour**. **Browse the user guides** opens the guides. The Help page remains available after the **Getting started** panel is dismissed.

<span id="faq-access-loading-and-display-errors"></span>

### Why is the page still loading, or why do I see “Something went wrong”?

Wait while company data loads; an incomplete load is not an empty schedule. If an unexpected display error replaces the page, select **Reload** to reopen the application. If the application cannot connect to the server, select **Try again**. If the active company fails to load, select **Retry**. Only the demo offers **Choose another company** on that screen. If the problem continues, contact whoever manages your CapacityLens service.

<span id="faq-access-planning-errors"></span>

### What should I do if a schedule read or save fails?

Read the notice. A failed schedule read blocks planning changes until a fresh data load succeeds. Failed or uncertain saves stay visible, but have no **Try again** action. A bar in the schedule does not prove the server saved it. The page may keep a planned change in memory while it stays open, but a reload cannot recover unsaved planning data from browser storage. Check the schedule before repeating an uncertain change; ask your Owner or Admin if the result remains unclear. [When something goes wrong](../docs/self-hosting/incidents.html) is for whoever manages the service.

<span id="faq-access-action-notices"></span>

### Why did an action notice disappear, or why is an error still showing?

Informational notices clear automatically so you can continue working. Error notices remain until you close them. If you are unsure whether a change succeeded, check the relevant page before submitting it again.

<span id="faq-access-appearance-and-colours"></span>

### Can I change the appearance, and what do button colours mean?

Open **Settings → My display** to choose **Light**, **Dark** or **Match system** for this device. Button colours distinguish product, positive, neutral, warning and destructive actions. Read the button label as well as its colour, especially before an action that removes or changes information.

<span id="faq-access-demo-companies"></span>

### Can I try another company or make a temporary one?

The company picker and temporary company creation are available in the demo only. In that demo, use the picker to switch companies or choose **New company** to create one; the sidebar also has **Switch company**. Demo changes exist only in memory. Reloading returns the demo to its seeded sample, so do not use it to keep company plans. A persisted installation is for one company and does not have a company switcher.

<span id="faq-access-browser-storage"></span>

### What if CapacityLens cannot read browser data?

If the browser-data recovery screen appears, select **Download raw copy** first, then check that the file appears in your downloads. This copies legacy or other local browser data; it does not contain or restore the server schedule. If you decide to discard unreadable local data, select **Reset data**, review **Reset stored data?**, and confirm with **Reset**. Cancel if you are not ready. If the download or reset fails, follow the message on screen and ask your administrator for help.

## Signing in and account access

<span id="faq-access-sign-in-and-page-not-found"></span>

### How do I sign in, or get back from a page-not-found message?

On the sign-in page, enter the email address and password for your account. Persisted company data requires you to sign in and have company access; the temporary demo does not use a real account. If an application page says **Page not found**, select **Go to schedule** to return to the application. If you followed an invitation or password-reset link, use that link’s instructions instead.

<span id="faq-access-account-details"></span>

### How can I check which account I’m using?

Open **Account** at the bottom of the left menu to see your name and email. Hover over or focus a shortened email to read it in full; scroll the row sideways on a narrow screen.

<span id="faq-access-password-recovery"></span>

### How do I change or reset my password?

In **Account**, select **Change password** and enter your current password, then a new password twice. New passwords must be 15–128 characters. A successful change signs you out of every browser and device. If the result is unclear, try signing in with the new password before repeating the change. If you forgot your password, select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and follow the emailed link. A reset also ends every session. If the email does not arrive, ask whoever manages the service to check delivery.

<span id="faq-access-sign-out"></span>

### How do I sign out?

Open **Account** and select **Sign out**. This ends only the current browser session; it does not remove your company membership or schedule records.

<span id="faq-access-invitation-and-limited-access"></span>

### Why can’t I open the company after accepting an invitation?

Accepting an invitation creates your sign-in and company membership and confirms your email address, but an Owner or Admin must still approve you before company data opens. Follow the access screen. Select **Ask for access** when shown; after approval, select **Check again**, then **Join company** if offered. If your email domain is refused, sign out and use your work address; ask an Owner or Admin about declined or withdrawn access. For an expired or used invitation, ask its sender for a new one. Membership and a scheduled Resource row are separate; an Owner or Admin can link them. Linking is optional; a visible row’s avatar opens its work list.

<span id="faq-access-email-confirmation"></span>

### How do I confirm my email address?

Select **Email me a confirmation link** and open the message. Follow its link, choose a password of 15–128 characters, then select **Confirm address**. On success, sign in with that password; confirmation ends your current sessions. If the link expired, sign in and request a new one. If CapacityLens cannot check the link, select **Try again**. If the result is unclear, try signing in with the new password; if it does not work, use your previous password and ask for another link. If email delivery is unavailable, contact your administrator; the screen explains when they need to confirm the address.

<span id="faq-access-session-check"></span>

### What if CapacityLens cannot verify my session?

The **Unable to verify your session** screen means the app could not check whether your sign-in is still valid. Select **Try again** when the service is available. If the check continues to fail, the error remains visible; contact whoever manages the service. A successful check returns you to the appropriate signed-in or sign-in screen.

<span id="faq-access-delete-account"></span>

### What happens if I delete my account?

Open **Account** and select **Delete account**. Enter your current password and confirm. Deletion removes your sign-in, sessions and company memberships, and cannot be undone. Your name remains on past schedules; an Owner or Admin can anonymise the scheduled person record separately. If you are the company Owner, transfer ownership before deleting your account.

## Schedule and capacity

<span id="faq-plan-navigate-schedule"></span>

### How do I move through the Schedule?

Use **Prev** and **Next** to move the window by one week, or select **Today** to return to the current date. **Weeks visible** offers 1, 2, 4, 6, or 8 weeks. The timeline aligns to your company’s configured week start when you change the range or scroll. To jump to a date, press **⌘K** on Mac or **Ctrl+K** on Windows, type a valid date in `YYYY-MM-DD` format (for example, `2026-11-23`), then choose **Go to date 2026-11-23**. The toolbar itself has no date picker.

<span id="faq-plan-read-capacity-cues"></span>

### What do the Schedule’s capacity cues mean?

When **Use disciplines** is on, resource rows are grouped by discipline. When disciplines are off, people share one Resources group unless there are Supplementary people; then engagement separates Studio and Supplementary groups. Their utilisation summaries describe the visible date range; changing **Weeks visible** changes that range. A red utilisation flag is different: it warns about over-capacity in the next 14 days, even when you are viewing another period. A day is over capacity only when booked work exceeds that day’s available capacity. A visible-window average below 100% can still include an overloaded day; check the red daily bands.

<span id="faq-plan-read-bars-and-days"></span>

### How can I tell work, time off, and unavailable days apart?

Work appears as labelled bars. Tentative, confirmed, and completed work have different visual treatments, and a note marker appears when a booking has a note. Time off appears as a labelled hatched block. Non-working and time-off days are shaded when the Schedule is zoomed in closely enough to show day-level detail. A vertical line marks today only while today is in view.

<span id="faq-plan-create-work"></span>

### How do I start a new booking from the Schedule?

Editors, Admins, and Owners can open the filter row with **Show filters** and choose **Work**, then drag across empty space in a resource’s row. The **New allocation** form opens with that resource and date range filled in. The **+** on a resource row also opens a form for the selected mode: **Work** opens **New allocation**, while **Time off** opens the time-off form. The **+** preselects that resource and starts from the left edge of the visible timeline. Review the form before saving.

<span id="faq-plan-create-time-off"></span>

### How do I add time off from the Schedule?

Editors, Admins, and Owners can open the filter row with **Show filters** and choose **Time off**. Then drag across the resource’s row for the dates away. The time-off form opens with that resource and date range filled in. This mode starts a time-off entry even when work bars cross the area you drag across.

<span id="faq-plan-change-allocation"></span>

### How do I change a booking’s dates or assignee?

An Editor, Admin or Owner can drag the body of a booking’s bar along its current row to move its dates. Drag either edge to change its date range; a booking must remain at least one day long. To change who it is assigned to, drag the bar to another resource’s row. If that destination is a placeholder for a different project, the change is rejected, the booking stays where it was, and the Schedule explains why.

<span id="faq-plan-undo-redo"></span>

### How do I undo or redo a planning change?

Editors, Admins, and Owners can use **Undo** or **Redo** in the toolbar, or press **⌘Z** / **Ctrl+Z** to undo and **⌘⇧Z** / **Ctrl+Shift+Z** to redo. A toolbar button is disabled when there is no change to reverse or reapply. Shortcuts do not run while you are typing, while text composition is active, or while a dialog owns input. Undo history lasts while this page stays open; reloading or another person’s change can clear it. It does not follow you to another device.

<span id="faq-plan-overlapping-bars"></span>

### Why are some bookings shown on separate lines?

Bookings that overlap in time for the same person are stacked into separate lanes so each remains visible. Bookings that do not overlap can share a lane. This layout changes how bars are drawn; it does not create additional resources or change the plan.

<span id="faq-plan-inspect-booking"></span>

### How can I read a booking without opening its form?

Hover over a booking or move keyboard focus to it to show its detail popover. It includes the available dates, status, and notes. A person’s avatar opens a read-only side sheet with their dated work and time off for the current company week and the following three weeks; closing it returns you to the avatar you opened.

<span id="faq-plan-overview-period-and-display"></span>

### Who can open Overview, and how do I change what it shows?

Overview access is set by an Owner or Admin. It defaults to Owners and Admins, with options for **Admins**, **Admins & Editors**, or **Everyone**. If the page is missing, ask your Owner or Admin to check this setting; being able to read Schedule does not automatically grant Overview access.

On Overview, choose 4, 8, or 12 weeks. It starts at 4 weeks each time you open the page, and its first column covers the remainder of the current week. Choose **Ledger** or **Load curve** to change how cells display capacity. **Tentative**, **Has availability**, and **Totals** control whether tentative work, people with availability, and weekly totals are included in the display; they start on, off, and off respectively. You can collapse discipline groups. When placeholders are enabled, they appear as unassigned demand.

Schedule utilisation follows its visible date range, while its red warning always looks 14 days ahead. Overview uses its own capacity display and filters. Its **Tentative**, **Has availability**, and **Totals** controls change displayed demand, people, and weekly summaries, so its figures are not directly interchangeable with Schedule’s.

## Find and filter work

<span id="faq-plan-search-people"></span>

### How do I find a person on Schedule?

Open the filter row with **Show filters**, then type a name or role in **Search people…**. The Schedule shows matching resource rows. Clear the search text to restore the rows that match your other active filters.

<span id="faq-plan-filter-discipline"></span>

### How do I show one discipline at a time?

When the company has **Use disciplines** enabled, use **Filter by discipline** in the filter row to choose a discipline. The Schedule shows that discipline’s resource group. Choose **All disciplines** to show the groups again. You can also select a discipline heading to collapse its rows; select it again to expand them.

<span id="faq-plan-filter-client-project"></span>

### How do client and project filters work together?

Choose a client in **Filter by client** to show that client’s bookings. Then use **Filter by project** to narrow the results; the project choices follow the selected client. A client or project filter changes which bars are visible, but capacity figures still include all work. Select **Clear Filters** to return to the full schedule.

<span id="faq-plan-filter-activity"></span>

### How do I find work by activity?

Use **Filter by activity** in the expanded filter row to focus on a type of work, including internal or all-projects activities. The filter narrows the visible work but does not recalculate capacity from only the matching bars. **Show unallocated** can add resources with no matching work to the results.

<span id="faq-plan-hide-tentative"></span>

### Can I hide tentative bookings from view?

Select **Tentative** to show tentative bars; select it again to hide them. Hiding those bars changes what you see, not the capacity calculation: tentative bookings still count toward capacity.

<span id="faq-plan-show-unallocated"></span>

### How do I see people without work matching my filter?

After you choose a client, project, or activity filter, check **Show unallocated** in the expanded filter row. Resources without matching work appear as dimmed rows, with capacity still based on all of their work. This option is available only while one of those work filters is active.

<span id="faq-plan-clear-filters"></span>

### How do I get back to the full Schedule after filtering?

Select **Clear Filters** to reset the search, selection filters, **Tentative**, and **Show unallocated** together. If your filters match no resources, the Schedule shows an empty state with a **Clear filters** action. Clearing the filters restores the rows.

<span id="faq-plan-filter-row-hidden"></span>

### I can’t see the filters. Where did they go?

Select **Show filters** to open the filter row. It contains search, client, project and activity filters, plus **Clear Filters**. The discipline filter appears when the company uses disciplines; Editors, Admins and Owners also see the **Work** / **Time off** draw-mode controls. Select **Hide filters** to collapse the row. Hiding it removes the controls from view but does not itself clear the current selections.

## Keyboard and form help

<span id="faq-plan-keyboard-bars"></span>

### Can I use booking bars without dragging?

Yes. Focus a booking bar with the keyboard. Its accessible name identifies it; press **Enter** to open editing when you have permission. Available arrow-key commands move or resize the bar. Viewers can inspect the Schedule but cannot make planning changes.

<span id="faq-plan-keyboard-dialogs"></span>

### What happens to keyboard focus when a dialog opens?

When a dialog opens, focus moves into it and stays within the dialog as you press **Tab**. Press **Escape** to close it where that action is supported, or use its close control. Focus returns to the control that opened the dialog.

<span id="faq-plan-form-errors"></span>

### How do I find and correct a form error?

An invalid field is marked and connected to its visible error message, so assistive technology can identify both together. Correct the field and its error association clears. If a form will not save, review the marked fields and their messages before trying again.

<span id="faq-plan-read-grid-with-screen-reader"></span>

### Can I understand the Schedule with a screen reader?

With a screen reader, you can move through resource rows and dates and hear applicable capacity and time-off details. You can also focus a booking bar to hear its details; press **Enter** to open editing when your access allows it.

<span id="faq-plan-form-width"></span>

### Why do form labels move above their fields?

On wider screens, management forms align labels beside their fields. At narrower widths, labels stack above the fields to keep them associated and readable. Required and conditional fields remain labelled. If a field is unclear, use its visible label and any validation message rather than relying on its position alone.

<span id="faq-plan-report-accessibility-problem"></span>

### How do I report an accessibility problem?

Use the support route your company gave you. Name the screen and control, describe what you were trying to do, and say where you got stuck. If relevant, include the keyboard or assistive technology you were using and any error message shown. This helps identify the task without assuming the same issue occurs on every screen.

## People and schedule rows

<span id="faq-people-add-person-and-sign-in"></span>

### How do I add someone to Schedule, and do they need a sign-in?

An Editor, Admin or Owner can open **Resources** and select **Add resource**. Enter their name, then set a job **Role**, working pattern and other details you use. The **Role** here describes their work; it is separate from their Viewer, Editor, Admin or Owner access. A resource row does not need a sign-in. If the teammate also needs access, an Owner or Admin invites them in **Team & access**. Linking the member to the resource is optional and connects the sign-in to that schedule row. See [Resources](../docs/using/resources.html).

<span id="faq-people-edit-resource"></span>

### How do I change a person's name or schedule details?

An Editor or above can select the edit control in a **Resources** row, change the fields and save. The form can change their name, job role, engagement, discipline, working days and availability dates. People must keep a name and at least one full or half working day. Changing their pattern or dates recalculates capacity but does not move their existing bookings.

<span id="faq-people-working-days-capacity"></span>

### How do working days affect capacity?

An Editor or above can edit the person in **Resources** and set **Working days** for each weekday. **Full day** is eight hours, **Half day** is four and **Not working** is zero. Capacity counts a day only when both the company and the person mark it as working. There is no separate editable hours-per-day field. If you change a pattern, existing bookings stay on their dates; review any newly over-capacity days.

<span id="faq-people-availability-dates"></span>

### Can I limit when a person is available?

An Editor or above can set **Start date** and/or **End date** in the person's **Resources** form. Both are optional and inclusive. Capacity is zero outside the dates, but existing bookings remain visible and are not moved or removed. To book new work outside the range, update the availability dates first.

<span id="faq-people-engagement"></span>

### What do Studio and Supplementary mean?

An Editor or above can choose **Engagement** in the person's **Resources** form. **Studio** and **Supplementary** place people in different roster sections; they do not change the person's working days or how capacity is counted.

<span id="faq-people-discipline-and-colour"></span>

### Why can't I set a person's colour in Resources?

A person's schedule colour comes from their **Discipline**, not a personal colour field. An Editor, Admin or Owner can add or edit a discipline on **Disciplines** and choose one of its preset colours. A person without a discipline uses the neutral colour. The Discipline field is available when the company has turned on **Use disciplines** and has at least one discipline.

<span id="faq-people-resource-list"></span>

### Why are people split into different sections on Resources?

Resources groups people by engagement once the company has a Supplementary person. Placeholders and external parties have separate sections when those features are on. Archived resources are below the active list. If an expected section is missing, check **Settings → Company features** for **Show placeholders** or **Show external resources**.

<span id="faq-people-favourite-resource"></span>

### How do I keep a resource near the top of the list?

An Editor or above can select the star in a person or external party row to mark it as a favourite. Favourites appear first in their section and the choice is shared with the company. The star is not available on placeholders or to Viewers.

<span id="faq-people-placeholder"></span>

### What is a placeholder, and when should I use one?

A placeholder reserves planned work for a role that is not assigned to a named person yet. An Editor or above can turn on **Show placeholders** in **Settings → Company features**, then select **Add placeholder** in **Resources**. A placeholder must be bound to a project; its name is optional. It follows company working days and has no personal pattern or availability dates. When you assign the work, edit the bookings to choose the person.

<span id="faq-people-external-party"></span>

### How do I list or update an outside company?

An Editor or above can turn on **Show external resources** in **Settings → Company features**. In **Resources**, use **Add external party** to enter the company name and an optional descriptor; edit its row to change either. External parties appear in the schedule for handoffs, but they have no capacity and do not contribute to utilisation.

<span id="faq-people-archive-resource"></span>

### What happens when I archive or restore a resource?

An Editor or above can archive a resource from its **Resources** row. Archiving hides the row from active scheduling while retaining its bookings and time off. Use **Restore** in the archived resources area to return it. Owners and Admins can see the full inactive-data view; Editors can restore eligible resources from the limited archive view.

<span id="faq-people-anonymise-resource"></span>

### Can I remove a former person's details but keep their planning history?

An Owner or Admin can select **Anonymise** for an archived resource. After confirmation, CapacityLens removes the person's identifying details and notes on their bookings and time off, while keeping the resource and its planning links. Anonymising cannot be undone. This action is available only for archived resources.

## Clients and projects

<span id="faq-people-client-project-create-edit"></span>

### How do clients and projects fit together?

A project belongs to one client. Add a client on **Clients**, then select **Add project** on **Projects** and choose that client. An Editor or above can add or edit either. Client forms include a name and preset colour; project forms include a name, client and preset colour. Existing bookings use the updated details after you save. See [Clients and projects](../docs/using/projects.html).

<span id="faq-people-internal-client"></span>

### Where do I find the Internal client?

**Internal** is the built-in client and does not appear in **Clients** management. The booking form’s **Project** menu includes **Internal** as a scope for internal work; it is not an Internal project record. An **Internal** activity is projectless: its form has no project picker and it saves without project attribution. Internal work remains visible and filterable for everyone and uses neutral grey styling.

<span id="faq-people-code-names"></span>

### Who can set or see a client or project code name?

Only the Owner can turn on **Use code name** and enter the required code name when adding or editing a client or project. Everyone below Owner sees the code name in place of the real name; only the Owner sees the real name. A code name keeps the work identifiable to the team without showing the client or project name.

<span id="faq-people-archive-client"></span>

### What does archiving a client do, and how do I restore it?

An Editor or above can choose **Archive** on a client row and confirm **Archive client?** Its projects and their bookings leave active views, but remain stored. In **Archived clients**, select **Restore** to bring the client back. Restoring a client reveals descendants that remain active; restore any separately archived project or activity on its own.

<span id="faq-people-archive-project"></span>

### What does archiving a project do?

An Editor, Admin or Owner can choose **Archive** on the project row and confirm **Archive project?** Its bookings leave Schedule, including bookings against shared All-projects activities that were attributed to this project. The records remain stored. An Editor or above can restore the project from **Archived projects**; any separately archived child still needs its own restore action.

<span id="faq-people-restore-project"></span>

### How do I bring an archived project back?

With Editor access or above, open **Projects** and find **Archived projects**, then select **Restore** on the project. Its active bookings return to active views. If the project is hidden because its client is archived, select **View archived parent**, then find and restore the client.

<span id="faq-people-phases-unavailable"></span>

### Can I add project phases or assign activities to one?

Phase controls are not currently available in the project or activity forms. Existing phase information remains in the company's data, but there is no current screen for adding or changing it. Use clear project and activity names to distinguish work stages.

## Activities and disciplines

<span id="faq-people-add-activity"></span>

### Which activity kind should I choose?

An Editor or above can open **Activities**, select **Add activity**, enter a name and choose **Kind**. Use **Project-specific** for work tied to one project; it requires that project. Use **All projects** for work that can be booked under several real projects or with **No specific project**. Use **Internal** for projectless internal work. The booking form’s **Project** menu has an **Internal** choice for that scope; no Internal project record is required. See [Activities](../docs/using/activities.html).

<span id="faq-people-edit-activity"></span>

### Can I change an activity's name or kind?

An Editor or above can select **Edit** on the activity row, change its name or kind, and save. **Project-specific** activities also require a project. If deleted items are still available to restore, changing the kind shows **This change clears N restorable items**. This includes restorable items unrelated to the activity. Select **Cancel** to keep them, or **Save change** to clear them; they cannot be restored or brought back with Undo afterward.

<span id="faq-people-archive-activity"></span>

### What happens when I archive an activity?

An Editor or above can select **Archive** on the activity row and confirm **Archive activity?** Its bookings are hidden from active views with the activity. The archived activity remains available in the archived activities area, where an Editor or above can select **Restore** to bring it and its active bookings back.

<span id="faq-people-restore-activity"></span>

### Why is an activity hidden, and how do I restore it?

An Editor, Admin or Owner can restore activities. If the activity itself was archived, find it under **Archived activities** and select **Restore**. If it is hidden only because its project or client is archived, select **View archived parent**, then find and restore that parent. Restoring a parent reveals children that were not archived separately.

<span id="faq-people-disciplines-order"></span>

### How do I add or change a discipline, and how are they ordered?

An Editor or above can add a discipline on **Disciplines** by entering a name and choosing a preset colour. If **Disciplines** is missing, an Editor or above can turn on **Use disciplines** in **Settings → Company features**. Use the edit control to change a discipline's name or colour; its schedule group position stays the same. The management list is alphabetical, while Schedule follows its saved group order. New disciplines are added after the existing schedule groups and appear once assigned to someone. See [Disciplines](../docs/using/disciplines.html).

<span id="faq-people-delete-discipline"></span>

### What happens if I delete a discipline?

An Editor or above can confirm **Delete discipline?** to remove the group. Its people are not deleted; they become ungrouped and keep their bookings. Select **Undo** if you removed it by mistake and want to restore the discipline and reconnect people who are still ungrouped.

## Bookings

<span id="faq-book-start-allocation"></span>

### How do I book work for someone?

On **Schedule**, select **Show filters** and choose **Work** if time-off drawing is selected. Choose the person’s row and select **+**, or drag across empty dates on that row. **New allocation** opens for that person. Choose a **Project** and **Activity**, check the dates and amount, then select **Save**. The saved booking appears on that person’s row. Only Editors, Admins and Owners can book work.

<span id="faq-book-amount-and-dates"></span>

### Why do I see different date and amount fields?

The company’s scheduling mode controls the form. In **Hours** mode, enter literal **Start** and **End** dates and choose **Hours / day**. In **Days** mode, enter **Days of work** and **Days over**; **End** is derived from the span. **Blocks** also derives the end from **Days over**, but does not measure workload or utilisation. Keep the company’s chosen mode; don’t change it just to use a different form.

<span id="faq-book-edit-cancel"></span>

### How do I change a booking or leave it as it is?

An Editor, Admin or Owner can select the booking bar to open **Edit allocation**. Its **Assignee**, project, activity, dates and other saved values are filled in. Make the change and select **Save** to update that booking. Select **Cancel** or the dialog’s close control to leave without saving; the existing booking stays as it was. If the schedule changed while the form was open, close it, reopen the booking, review the current values and apply your change again.

<span id="faq-book-duplicate"></span>

### What does **Duplicate** do?

For an eligible booking, an Editor, Admin or Owner can use **Duplicate** to create a separate copy with the same person, project, activity and planning details. The original stays in place; use **Undo** on Schedule if you duplicated it by mistake. Repeated bookings cannot be duplicated from an individual occurrence.

<span id="faq-book-delete-allocation"></span>

### How do I remove a booking, and can I undo it?

An Editor, Admin or Owner can select **Delete**, which asks for confirmation. If the booking repeats, choose whether to remove only this occurrence or this and future occurrences. Cancel the confirmation to keep the booking. After confirming, use **Undo** on Schedule to restore a mistaken deletion during the current page session.

<span id="faq-book-inline-activity"></span>

### The activity I need is missing. Can I add it from the booking form?

If the company has enabled **Inline activity creation**, enter a name in the new activity field and select **Add activity**. The activity is created in the scope selected in the form and becomes the booking’s activity. If that control is not available, an Editor, Admin or Owner can add it on **Activities**; inline creation is a company setting, not a per-booking option.

<span id="faq-book-change-assignee"></span>

### How do I give a booking to someone else?

An Editor, Admin or Owner can drag a booking onto another person’s row. Normally this keeps its start and number of working days, so the end can change with the new pattern. With **Ignore working days**, the span uses calendar days; external-party bookings use literal dates. Or open the bar and change **Assignee** in **Edit allocation**. In that form, **Days** and **Blocks** derive the end from **Days over**, while **Hours** keeps the selected **Start** and **End** dates. Check the destination’s working pattern and availability dates. CapacityLens does not choose a replacement person for you.

<span id="faq-book-placeholder-project"></span>

### Why can’t I change the project for a placeholder booking?

A placeholder is tied to one **Bound project**. Its booking stays with that project, and the **Activity** choices are limited to activities for that project and shared **All projects** activities. Choose a different placeholder or a person resource if the work belongs to another project.

<span id="faq-book-save-errors"></span>

### Why won’t the booking form save?

Fix marked fields: choose an activity and valid dates, with **Hours / day** or **Days of work** above zero. New bookings must start on a working day outside time off or a closure. If the schedule changed, reopen and reapply.

The **Changes aren’t being saved right now — we’ll keep retrying** banner means the save is unconfirmed. The row may update first. Retries continue in memory while open; reloading loses that state, so there is no offline recovery. Verify the plan after the banner clears. If unclear, check Schedule before retrying; it may have saved.

<span id="faq-book-repeat-patterns"></span>

### Do repeating bookings and repeating time off work the same way?

No. Bookings can repeat weekly, every two, three or four weeks, or monthly. Set **Repeat until** no more than six calendar months after the start; the preview shows linked bookings, and one **Undo** removes the batch. Later, edit an occurrence on its own or delete it with future occurrences. Repeating time off has a finite preview and cutoff; the cutoff includes an entry that starts on that date. After saving, each time-off entry is independent, with no continuing series. A Viewer asks a planner to enter time off.

<span id="faq-book-task-field"></span>

### Where is the optional **Task** field?

The one-line **Task** field appears in the booking form only when an Editor, Admin or Owner has enabled **Show task field in schedule** in company settings. It adds context to the booking; it does not replace the required **Activity** or turn CapacityLens into a task list. If the field is absent, leave it out or ask a planner whether the company needs it.

## Time off and closures

<span id="faq-book-add-time-off"></span>

### How do I record someone’s time off?

On **Time off**, select **Add time off**. Choose the **Resource**, **Start**, **End** and **Type**—**Holiday**, **Sick**, **Unpaid** or **Other**—then select **Save**. Time off uses whole days; the dates appear in the list and as a hatched block on Schedule. Only an Editor, Admin or Owner can add or change time off. Viewers cannot submit a holiday request in CapacityLens; ask the person who plans the schedule to enter it.

<span id="faq-book-edit-delete-time-off"></span>

### Can I correct or remove a time-off entry?

An Editor, Admin or Owner can use **Edit** beside the entry to update its dates, type or note, then save. Only Owners and Admins can see or edit time-off notes. When an entry came from a repeat, editing it changes only that entry. **Delete** asks for confirmation; cancel to keep it, or confirm to remove it from the list and Schedule. A mistaken deletion can be restored with **Undo** during the current page session.

<span id="faq-book-time-off-type"></span>

### Why doesn’t the Time off list show whether it is holiday or sick time?

The list is designed to scan who is away and for how long. The type label appears on the matching time-off block on **Schedule**. Open that view to distinguish **Holiday**, **Sick**, **Unpaid** or **Other**.

<span id="faq-book-old-time-off"></span>

### An old absence disappeared from the list. Was it deleted?

No. **Time off** lists entries that end in the current company week or later. Past entries are hidden from this forward-looking list but remain in the schedule data. Current and upcoming personal time off is grouped under each resource.

<span id="faq-book-company-closures"></span>

### Who can add or change a company closure?

An Editor, Admin or Owner can open **Time off → Company closures** and select **Add closure**. Enter its **Name**, **Start** and **End**, then save. To change one, select **Edit**, update the fields and save. A closure makes every person and placeholder unavailable on those dates; existing bookings stay visible so you can review any overlap. Closures are one-off dates, not repeating personal time off.

<span id="faq-book-delete-closure"></span>

### What happens when I delete a company closure?

An Editor, Admin or Owner can select **Delete** beside the closure and confirm. Cancel to keep it. Once removed, the closure band disappears from Schedule and people become available where their usual working days and other absences allow. Existing bookings keep their dates; deleting the closure does not move them.

## Settings and access

<span id="faq-company-company-details"></span>

### Can I change the company name, week start or time zone in Settings?

No. **Settings → Data and support → Company details** shows these values as a read-only summary for every role. The company name, week start and time zone are selected during company setup; there is no current Settings control for changing them later. The time zone determines which date the app treats as today. [Settings](../docs/using/settings.html#calendar) explains the summary.

<span id="faq-company-my-display"></span>

### Which display choices affect only my browser?

Open **Settings → My display** to choose whether schedule bars show client and project names, minimise weekend columns, and show total, discipline or personal utilisation figures. These switches start on; the discipline figure appears only when the company uses disciplines. The choices apply to this browser and do not change the company's plan or appear in an export. Your appearance choice is also per device. Weekend columns narrow only at day-level zoom.

<span id="faq-company-schedule-behavior"></span>

### Can I change date formatting or make weekends narrower?

**Minimise weekends** is under **Settings → My display → Schedule on this device** and changes only your browser's grid. Schedule aligns to the company's week boundaries automatically; there is no setting to turn that behavior on or off. Company date format is under **Settings → Company setup** and is shared by everyone. Editors and above can choose **9 Sep**, **9th Sep**, **Sep 9** or **Sep 9th**. [Settings](../docs/using/settings.html) lists the personal and company choices.

<span id="faq-company-company-planning-settings"></span>

### Which shared planning settings can an Editor change?

Editors, Admins and Owners can change **Company setup** choices such as **Allocation units**, **Company-wide working days** and **Date format**. **Hours** asks for hours per day, **Days** for work days spread over a span, and **Blocks** for a span without measuring workload. Changing units does not rewrite existing bookings. Working-day changes apply immediately, recalculate capacity and conflicts, and leave booking dates where they are. At least one company working day must remain selected.

<span id="faq-company-overview-access"></span>

### Why can’t I open Overview?

Overview access defaults to **Admins** (Owners and Admins). An Owner or Admin can change **Settings → Company setup → Overview access** to **Admins & Editors** or **Everyone**. The choice controls who can open Overview, not who can see the schedule data. It does not make bookings or resources private from other company members.

<span id="faq-company-scheduling-features"></span>

### What happens when a company feature is turned off?

Editors, Admins and Owners can change the **Company features** switches in **Settings → Scheduling features**. **Use disciplines**, **Show placeholders**, **Show external resources**, **Inline activity creation** and **Show task field in schedule** affect the whole company. Turning a switch off hides its controls or rows; it does not delete existing records or task text. New production companies start with these features off; the demo starts with **Use disciplines** on. Viewers can read the settings but cannot change them.

<span id="faq-company-internal-work-colour"></span>

### Can I choose a colour for Internal work?

No. Internal projects and activities use neutral grey automatically. There is no colour setting for Internal work. Use the project or activity colours for client work instead.

<span id="faq-company-diagnostics"></span>

### Where can I find build details or send a problem report?

Owners and Admins can open **Diagnostics**. Select **Copy diagnostics** to copy the support report; the page confirms the copy or reports if the browser blocks it. **Build details** appears when the installation provides build information; **Send feedback** appears only when a valid feedback address is configured and opens a preaddressed email draft. If an option is missing, that information or address is not configured. [Diagnostics](../docs/admin/diagnostics.html) explains what the report contains.

<span id="faq-company-password-reset"></span>

### What if I forgot my password?

On the sign-in page, select **Forgot password?** and follow the emailed reset link. If the email does not arrive, ask your installation operator to check mail delivery. Resetting your password ends every active session, including the one you are using, so you must sign in again on all browsers and devices. [Account](../docs/using/account.html#if-you-can-t-sign-in) has the recovery steps.

<span id="faq-company-archive-restore"></span>

### Where do I restore an archived person, client, project or activity?

An Editor or above can restore an eligible archived item on its own **Resources**, **Clients**, **Projects** or **Activities** page; it is not restored from Settings. Archiving a client, project or activity hides its related booking bars until restored. Archiving a person hides their row from Schedule while keeping their past bookings. An Owner or Admin can also anonymise an archived person; that removes identifying details and dependent notes and cannot be undone. Use [Anonymise all people](#faq-company-anonymise-all) for the separate company-wide action. [Resources](../docs/using/resources.html#archive-someone-who-leaves) and [Clients and projects](../docs/using/projects.html#archive-or-restore-a-client-or-project) explain their archive views.

<span id="faq-company-transfer-ownership"></span>

### How does the Owner transfer company ownership?

Only the current Owner can transfer ownership. In **Team & access → Company ownership**, choose another active Admin, select **Transfer ownership**, then confirm with the current Owner's password. The selected Admin becomes Owner and the former Owner becomes Admin immediately. Closing or cancelling the confirmation leaves ownership unchanged. No one can become Owner through an invitation or ordinary role change.

<span id="faq-company-anonymise-all"></span>

### What does “Anonymise all people” remove?

On a server installation, only the Owner can use **Settings → Data and support → Anonymise all people**. After the confirmation, enter the Owner's current password to continue. This irreversible action removes identifying resource details and personal notes from bookings and time off across the company. It keeps the company, catalogue, memberships and planning links. It cannot be undone. In the temporary demo, it changes only in-memory data and does not ask for a password.

<span id="faq-company-check-role"></span>

### Where do I check my company role and permissions?

Open **Team & access → Your access** and select **See full capabilities**. Every role can check its own access; the demo shows a demo-access note instead of real membership permissions. Viewers can read the schedule; Editors can also change planning and ordinary company settings; Owners and Admins manage invitations and memberships. Only the Owner sees private client and project real names in ordinary company use. Ask an Owner or Admin if you need a different role.

## Teammates and invitations

<span id="faq-company-invite-teammate"></span>

### How does an Owner or Admin invite a teammate?

In **Team & access**, choose **Invite someone**, select **Editor**, **Viewer** or **Admin**, enter the teammate's email and choose **Create invite**. The app emails the invitation to that address and does not show its link; only that address can accept it. The default role is Editor; Owner is not offered. If mail delivery is unavailable, the invite cannot be created. If the invite was saved but email delivery failed, use **Resend** on that invitation after delivery is available.

<span id="faq-company-pending-invitation"></span>

### How do I resend or cancel an invitation?

An Owner or Admin can find unaccepted invitations under **Team & access → Outstanding invites**. **Resend** emails a fresh link and makes the earlier link stop working. **Revoke** cancels an invitation that has not expired; expired invitations have no Revoke action. Invitations expire after seven days. If the person already has an account, ask the person who manages the service to help that existing account join the company, rather than creating a second account.

<span id="faq-company-accept-invitation"></span>

### Does accepting an invitation give someone access to company data?

The invitation page shows the company and offered role. The invitee enters **Name** and **Password**, then selects **Accept invitation**. If the next screen offers **Ask for access**, the sign-in and membership exist, but the invitation alone has not granted access to company data. Select **Ask for access** and wait for an Owner or Admin to approve. An invitation does not create a schedule person.

<span id="faq-company-admission"></span>

### How does the invitee check whether access was approved?

When the access screen offers **Ask for access**, the invitee selects it and waits for an Owner or Admin. The approver opens **Team & access → Waiting for approval** and chooses **Approve** or **Decline**. The invitee selects **Check again** to refresh the result; access changes take effect on that next request. If **Join company** appears because the person has no membership yet, select it to finish joining. Some installations also restrict which email domains can join. If access was declined or revoked, contact an Owner or Admin; the screen has no request or refresh control then.

<span id="faq-company-member-role-or-status"></span>

### Can an Owner or Admin change a member's role or suspend them?

In **Team & access → Members**, use the member's actions to edit their role or choose **Suspend**. **Restore membership** returns a suspended person with their previous role. Suspension takes effect on their next request and keeps their role and history. Owners and Admins cannot change their own membership or the Owner's membership. An Admin cannot promote someone to Owner; ownership uses its separate transfer action.

<span id="faq-company-link-member-resource"></span>

### Does a teammate need a linked Resource to sign in?

No. A member can sign in and use the company without a schedule row linked to them. An Owner or Admin can optionally choose **Link to Resource** from the member row in **Team & access**, then select an available active person. Choosing or removing a link saves immediately; it connects the sign-in to an existing row but does not create a Resource or change the person's access role. Anyone who can see a row can open its avatar work list, linked or not.

<span id="faq-company-view-as-member"></span>

### What does “View the app as this member” do?

An Owner or Admin can select **View the app as this member** on another active member's row in **Team & access**. After confirmation, a banner identifies the member and shows when the view ends. It lasts 15 minutes; choose **End now** to return sooner. This view is read-only: it cannot change the plan, membership or company settings. The view applies to all signed-in sessions. Signing out ends it only for the current session; other active sessions keep it until it is ended or expires. If the member view needs restoring, choose **Retry** in the banner.

<span id="faq-company-sign-in-tracking"></span>

### Can I turn on member sign-in tracking?

There is no current **Record member sign-ins** switch or **Signed in** column in the product interface. The Owner cannot enable this from **Team & access**. Do not use a missing column as evidence that a member has or has not signed in.

## Company data and privacy

<span id="faq-company-export-data"></span>

### What does Export JSON include, and who can use it?

Any company member can select **Settings → Data and support → Export JSON**. The export contains company scheduling data and supported settings, not member accounts, sessions, invitations or this browser's display choices. The export follows the role you are currently viewing: Owner and Admin views include archived and deleted planning records; Editor and Viewer views include active records. Normally only the Owner receives private real names; an ordinary Admin export uses code names. An Admin deliberately viewing the app as the Owner also exports that Owner view, including real names. Keep an export protected like company data.

<span id="faq-company-import-data"></span>

### How do I preview and replace company data?

Only an Owner can import on a server installation. In **Settings → Data and support → Import JSON**, choose a full JSON export made by CapacityLens and select **Continue**. Review the company and record count, then choose **Replace data**. Server imports require the Owner's current password to confirm that exact file. Cancel or a rejected preview leaves company data unchanged; if company data changed after preview, preview again. A full export can restore planning to a prepared company; invitees and sign-ins must be set up separately.

<span id="faq-company-import-undo"></span>

### Can I undo an import?

No. A confirmed import replaces the selected company's planning data and settings, and it cannot be undone with Schedule **Undo** or **Redo**. Export the current company first if you may need to restore it. Imported data also starts without the previous page's Undo history.

<span id="faq-company-invalid-import"></span>

### Why was my import file rejected?

Import accepts only a valid, full CapacityLens export. A redacted export, another product's file or a malformed file is rejected before replacement, leaving current company data in place. Do not edit the JSON to try to repair it; export a fresh full file from CapacityLens and preview that file. If the export is redacted, only an Owner's full export can restore private names.

<span id="faq-company-save-and-reload"></span>

### Do saved changes remain after I reload?

On a server-backed company, a change remains after reload once the server has saved it. A bar that appears immediately is not proof that its save completed. The app may retry while the page remains open, but it does not save scheduling data in the browser for recovery after reload. If a save result is uncertain, inspect the latest company data before repeating the action because it may already have been saved.

<span id="faq-company-conflict"></span>

### What if another person changed the plan while I was editing?

That edit is refused and the app loads the latest company plan. Close and reopen the form, review the current values, then reapply your change. Your earlier edit was not saved. For a result that is uncertain rather than refused, check the refreshed plan before trying again so you do not duplicate a change that already committed. [Connection errors](../docs/using/settings.html#connection-errors) explains the visible save warning.

<span id="faq-company-example-data"></span>

### Can I add example data to an existing company?

Only Owners and Admins can add example data, and only to an empty server-backed company. **Add example data** creates two people, a client, a project and sample bookings. It is unavailable once planning records exist, including archived records. The built-in **Internal** client alone does not block this option. The new records are ordinary records that can be archived.

<span id="faq-company-demo-data"></span>

### What happens to demo changes when I leave or reload?

The demo is temporary and in memory. Your changes last only while the demo page remains open; reloading restores its original example data. Demo changes are not saved to a company server and are not a durable backup. Use a server-backed company for planning that must remain available after reload.

<span id="faq-company-private-names"></span>

### Who can see or change a private client or project name?

When **Use code name** is on, the Owner's ordinary view shows the real name; Admins, Editors and Viewers see the code name. An Admin who deliberately uses the read-only member view as the Owner sees the Owner's view. Only the Owner can change the privacy fields. An Admin or Editor can make permitted ordinary edits, but those edits preserve private fields they cannot see. [Privacy](../docs/security/privacy.html) explains the name projection.

## Installation and service

<span id="faq-access-installation-help"></span>

### Who should I contact to install or maintain CapacityLens?

CapacityLens needs a hosting operator to install and maintain the service. Before setup, arrange someone responsible for the running service, a public HTTPS address, email delivery for invitations and account recovery, and backups. The operator installs and opens the service; the first admitted person then enters the company name, chooses whether the week starts Monday or Sunday, and selects a time zone in the browser. That person becomes the company Owner. Company details are read-only after setup. Hosting maintenance and backups remain the operator’s responsibility. See [Install CapacityLens](../docs/self-hosting/install.html) for that route. If your agency already has an installation, ask its operator for help with service problems.
