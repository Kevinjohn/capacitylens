## Finding your way around

<span id="faq-access-find-sections"></span>

### Why can’t I see a section or open a page?

The left menu shows the sections available to your access. The current section is marked in the menu. You can also press **⌘K** on macOS or **Ctrl+K** on Windows or Linux to search for a page, person, project or date, then choose an available result. If a page address no longer works, return to **Schedule** from the not-found page. Ask your Owner or Admin if you think a section should be available to you.

<span id="faq-access-navigation-size"></span>

### Can I make more room for the schedule or use the menu on a narrow screen?

On a desktop, select **Collapse menu** in the sidebar header, or press **⌘B** on macOS or **Ctrl+B** on Windows or Linux. Select **Expand menu** to restore it. On a narrow screen, open the menu to see the available sections; choosing one closes the menu. The schedule supports viewing on a narrow screen, but mobile scheduling workflows are not provided.

<span id="faq-access-guidance"></span>

### Where can I get a tour or find help?

The first time you enter, CapacityLens may show a short introduction. Reopen it with **How CapacityLens works** in the sidebar. Owners, Admins and Editors see **Getting started**, with steps for preparing a useful schedule. Owners and Admins can dismiss it for the whole company. Editors can show or hide the panel, but cannot dismiss it for everyone. Viewers use **Help → Show tour** instead. For a tour suited to your role, open **Help** from the left menu and select **Show tour**. **Browse the user guides** opens the guides. The Help page remains available after the **Getting started** panel is dismissed.

<span id="faq-access-loading-and-display-errors"></span>

### Why is the page still loading, or why do I see “Something went wrong”?

Wait while company data loads; an incomplete load is not an empty schedule. If an unexpected display error replaces the page, select **Reload** to reopen the application. If the application cannot connect to the server, select **Try again**. If the active company fails to load, select **Retry**. Only the demo offers **Choose another company** on that screen. If the problem continues, contact whoever manages your CapacityLens service.

<span id="faq-access-planning-errors"></span>

### What should I do if a schedule read or save fails?

Read the notice. A failed schedule read blocks planning changes until a fresh data load succeeds. Failed or uncertain saves stay visible, but have no **Try again** action. A bar in the schedule does not prove the server saved it. The page may keep a planned change in memory while it stays open, but a reload cannot recover unsaved planning data from browser storage. Check the schedule before repeating an uncertain change; ask your Owner or Admin if the result remains unclear. [When something goes wrong](../../docs/self-hosting/incidents.html) is for whoever manages the service.

<span id="faq-access-action-notices"></span>

### Why did an action notice disappear, or why is an error still showing?

Informational notices clear automatically so you can continue working. Error notices remain until you close them. If you are unsure whether a change succeeded, check the relevant page before submitting it again.

<span id="faq-access-appearance-and-colours"></span>

### Can I change the appearance, and what do button colours mean?

Open **Settings → My display** to choose **Light**, **Dark** or **Match system** for this device. Button colours distinguish product, positive, neutral, warning and destructive actions. Read the button label as well as its colour, especially before an action that removes or changes information.

<span id="faq-access-demo-companies"></span>

### Can I try another company or make a temporary one?

The company picker and temporary company creation are available in the demo only. In that demo, use the picker to switch companies or choose **New company** to create one; the sidebar also has **Switch company**. Demo changes exist only in memory. Reloading returns the demo to its seeded sample, so do not use it to keep company plans. A persisted installation is for one company and does not have a company switcher.

<span id="faq-access-browser-storage"></span>

### What if CapacityLens cannot read browser data?

If the browser-data recovery screen appears, select **Download raw copy** first, then check that the file appears in your downloads. This copies legacy or other local browser data; it does not contain or restore the server schedule. If you decide to discard unreadable local data, select **Reset data**, review **Reset stored data?**, and confirm with **Reset**. Cancel if you are not ready. If the download or reset fails, follow the message on screen and ask your administrator for help.

## Signing in and account access

<span id="faq-access-sign-in-and-page-not-found"></span>

### How do I sign in, or get back from a page-not-found message?

On the sign-in page, enter the email address and password for your account. Persisted company data requires you to sign in and have company access; the temporary demo does not use a real account. If an application page says **Page not found**, select **Go to schedule** to return to the application. If you followed an invitation or password-reset link, use that link’s instructions instead.

<span id="faq-access-account-details"></span>

### How can I check which account I’m using?

Open **Account** at the bottom of the left menu to see your name and email. Hover over or focus a shortened email to read it in full; scroll the row sideways on a narrow screen.

<span id="faq-access-password-recovery"></span>

### How do I change or reset my password?

In **Account**, select **Change password** and enter your current password, then a new password twice. New passwords must be 15–128 characters. A successful change signs you out of every browser and device. If the result is unclear, try signing in with the new password before repeating the change. If you forgot your password, select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and follow the emailed link. A reset also ends every session. If the email does not arrive, ask whoever manages the service to check delivery.

<span id="faq-access-sign-out"></span>

### How do I sign out?

Open **Account** and select **Sign out**. This ends only the current browser session; it does not remove your company membership or schedule records.

<span id="faq-access-invitation-and-limited-access"></span>

### Why can’t I open the company after accepting an invitation?

Accepting an invitation creates your sign-in and company membership and confirms your email address, but an Owner or Admin must still approve you before company data opens. Follow the access screen. Select **Ask for access** when shown; after approval, select **Check again**, then **Join company** if offered. If your email domain is refused, sign out and use your work address; ask an Owner or Admin about declined or withdrawn access. For an expired or used invitation, ask its sender for a new one. Membership and a scheduled Resource row are separate; an Owner or Admin can link them. Linking is optional; a visible row’s avatar opens its work list.

<span id="faq-access-email-confirmation"></span>

### How do I confirm my email address?

Select **Email me a confirmation link** and open the message. Follow its link, choose a password of 15–128 characters, then select **Confirm address**. On success, sign in with that password; confirmation ends your current sessions. If the link expired, sign in and request a new one. If CapacityLens cannot check the link, select **Try again**. If the result is unclear, try signing in with the new password; if it does not work, use your previous password and ask for another link. If email delivery is unavailable, contact your administrator; the screen explains when they need to confirm the address.

<span id="faq-access-session-check"></span>

### What if CapacityLens cannot verify my session?

The **Unable to verify your session** screen means the app could not check whether your sign-in is still valid. Select **Try again** when the service is available. If the check continues to fail, the error remains visible; contact whoever manages the service. A successful check returns you to the appropriate signed-in or sign-in screen.

<span id="faq-access-delete-account"></span>

### What happens if I delete my account?

Open **Account** and select **Delete account**. Enter your current password and confirm. Deletion removes your sign-in, sessions and company memberships, and cannot be undone. Your name remains on past schedules; an Owner or Admin can anonymise the scheduled person record separately. If you are the company Owner, transfer ownership before deleting your account.

## Installation and service

<span id="faq-access-installation-help"></span>

### Who should I contact to install or maintain CapacityLens?

CapacityLens needs a hosting operator to install and maintain the service. Before setup, arrange someone responsible for the running service, a public HTTPS address, email delivery for invitations and account recovery, and backups. The operator installs and opens the service; the first admitted person then enters the company name, chooses whether the week starts Monday or Sunday, and selects a time zone in the browser. That person becomes the company Owner. Company details are read-only after setup. Hosting maintenance and backups remain the operator’s responsibility. See [Install CapacityLens](../../docs/self-hosting/install.html) for that route. If your agency already has an installation, ask its operator for help with service problems.
