FAQ story coverage ​

Maintainer reference: 179 of 179 stories map to 114 reader questions. Coverage means a linked answer represents each capability; it is not a claim that every acceptance criterion was exercised or that every feature is currently available.

The source is the expanded catalogue in PR #1848, pinned at 0d961752a0bb682b1348addf1ae621ae70abe57d (open proposal at the time of this snapshot). This exploration’s original checkout has 132 story files. The proposal’s additional stories are used as coverage prompts, not as permission to change the product or advertise unavailable controls.

Hidden, conditional and demo-only behaviour stays explicit in the answers. The builder refuses missing stories, duplicate mappings and missing question anchors. If the local catalogue gains an identifier absent from the snapshot, the build requires a catalogue refresh. Matching counts alone do not prove the answers are accurate; the content still needs source review.
