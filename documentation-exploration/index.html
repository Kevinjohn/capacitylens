Three ways to find your next step

One journey: the company is already set up, the team makes a feasible plan for next week, and a Viewer can find their work. Compare the writing and navigation before choosing a direction.

The selected direction is a task-led guide with an intention-led entrance and a comprehensive FAQ appendix. A is the foundation; B offers an optional walkthrough. C remains available for comparison.

All three options cover the same ten destinations. The sequence is optional: start at the task you need. These are documentation samples, not a new product interface.
