# Add schedule people

The **Resources** page holds the rows that receive work. A sign-in is a separate record: Bruce can be a Viewer and still have a schedule row, but one does not create the other.

Diana opens **Resources** and selects **Add resource** for Bruce. She enters **Name** as Bruce Wayne, uses **Role** for a job title such as Designer, and leaves **Engagement** at the appropriate team choice. **Role** here describes the schedule person’s job; it does not set Bruce’s Viewer access. She sets **Working days** to **Full day** Monday through Friday and **Not working** on the weekend, then selects **Save**. Start and end dates can stay blank for ongoing availability.

Diana adds herself as a resource in the same way if she may take work. That gives the planner a destination when Bruce’s Monday becomes full. The result is two separate rows on **Schedule**, each with its own working pattern.

## Optionally link Bruce’s sign-in to his row

Bruce can already open his visible row’s work list from its avatar. To also connect his membership with the person being scheduled, Diana opens **Team & access** and selects the link control on his member row. She chooses Bruce Wayne from the available resources. The link saves immediately; it does not change his Viewer role or make him an Editor.

Only an Owner or Admin can link a member to a resource. Each member and each resource can have one link. If Bruce’s row is not offered, check that it is active and not already linked to another member. An Editor can still create or edit schedule people, but needs an Owner/Admin for this account link.

Once both rows are available, use [Set up the work](04-add-work.md) to make the project and activity Diana will book.
