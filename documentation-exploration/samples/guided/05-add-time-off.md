# Record the absence

Bruce is unavailable on Friday next week. Diana records that first, so the plan starts from the time he can actually work.

On **Time off**, under **Personal time off**, Diana selects **Add time off**. She chooses Bruce in **Resource**, sets **Start** and **End** to Friday, chooses the appropriate **Type**, leaves **Repeat** as **Doesn’t repeat**, then selects **Save**. The date appears in the list and as a hatched block on Bruce’s row in **Schedule**.

For this example, no note is needed. Time-off notes are visible to Owners and Admins. Everyone else can see the dates, but not the note.

## If work later overlaps the absence

Time off and bookings remain separate records. CapacityLens keeps a booking in place if time off is added over it; it marks the overlap so a planner can decide what to change. Do not expect an absence to move or delete booked work automatically.

This plan keeps Friday clear and books Bruce Monday through Thursday. If that changes and work is added across his Friday off, review the red overlap on **Schedule** and [adjust the plan](08-resolve-overbooking.md). A Viewer does not have an in-product holiday request: Bruce asks Diana or another Editor/Admin/Owner to enter the absence for him.

Once the absence is visible, Diana can check where there is room before booking: [Find room in the week](06-find-capacity.md).
