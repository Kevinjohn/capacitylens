# Find room in the week

Before Diana books Bruce, she checks the week that contains Monday through Friday next week. On **Schedule**, she selects **Next** to move one week at a time, **Today** to return to the current week, and **Weeks visible** to focus the grid on one week. She can select **Show filters** to narrow by person, client, project, or activity. **Clear Filters** restores the full view.

The utilisation percentage beside a row covers the dates currently visible. Changing the week span can change that percentage. A red day marks work above that day’s available capacity. The separate red warning for overwork looks 14 days forward from today, even when the visible week changes. An average at or below 100% does not rule out an overloaded day; inspect the individual days as well.

An empty cell is not always free capacity. Working days, time off, company closures, and the person’s availability dates all affect whether work can start or how much capacity is available. Here Bruce’s Friday absence is already visible, so Diana plans around it.

## When Overview is available

**Overview** can compare free capacity one week at a time. By default only Owners and Admins can open it. An Owner or Admin can broaden access in **Settings → Overview access**; this changes who can open the page, not who can see schedule data. In **Overview**, **Has availability** narrows the table to people with room during the displayed weeks. Overview does not show capacity figures when the company plans in Blocks.

Diana can now make the first booking for Bruce: [Book the first work](07-book-work.md).
