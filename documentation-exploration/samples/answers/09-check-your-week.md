# How can I find my work as a Viewer?

Open **Schedule** and select the avatar beside your name to see your work list. It always covers the current company week and the next three weeks. It is independent of the grid's zoom, date position and filters, and includes bookings that may be hidden from the grid. The list also includes your personal time off; company closures and ordinary non-working days are omitted.

To find a date in the grid, select **Today** and set **Weeks visible** to include next week. Select **Show filters** and check the person search, client, project or activity filters. **Clear Filters** removes active filters. If your row is still missing, ask an Owner or Admin to check your access and the schedule row.

Your sign-in is separate from a schedule row. Until an Owner or Admin links them in **Team & access**, find your row by the resource name. Team & access shows your company role; a Viewer can read the schedule but cannot edit bookings, resources or time off.

Some client or project names appear in quotation marks. Those are code names for confidential work; only the Owner can see the real names. Time-off notes are visible only to Owners and Admins. A missing private name is expected access behavior.

If a booking is missing or incorrect, tell the person who plans the schedule what needs checking. CapacityLens has no share or publish command for sending a correction. A planner with Editor access or above can make the change.
