Resources

For: Editor, Admin, Owner

How do I add someone to the schedule? ​

An Editor, Admin or Owner can create schedule people. Open Resources, choose Add resource, and enter the person's Name. Set their Role (their work title, such as Designer), Engagement, and Working days. If they are available only for a limited period, set Start date and End date. Choose Save to add the row.

The resource Role describes their job; it is separate from their company access role. Match Working days to the person's usual pattern. A half-day affects their capacity, while a non-working day has none. Bookings outside availability dates remain visible for review and may show over capacity.

Does this create a sign-in? ​

No. A resource is a schedule row. If the person needs to sign in, an Owner or Admin invites them through Team & access. After they join, that Owner/Admin can select the link icon by their membership in Team & access and choose the resource row. Linking saves immediately; it does not change their access role. A person and a member can each have only one link.

Viewers cannot add or edit resources. If Add resource is unavailable, ask an Owner or Admin to assign Editor access. For an unfilled role, an Editor+ can add a placeholder instead; it has company working days and is bound to a project.
