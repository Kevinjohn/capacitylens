Schedule

For: Editor, Admin, Owner

How do I book work for next week? ​

An Editor, Admin or Owner can open Schedule, create a New allocation on a person's row, fill in the work and dates, then save it.

  1. On Schedule, choose Work in the filter row if time-off drawing is selected, then choose + beside the person, or drag across empty dates. The New allocation form opens.
  2. Choose the Project and Activity, check you opened the intended person's row, set the start date and enter the amount for your company's scheduling mode.
  3. Choose Save and confirm the booking appears on the person's row.

For a concrete example, use a company set to Monday–Friday working days, full working days, Days mode and no prior work. Enter:

  • Project: Website refresh (Wayne Enterprises)
  • Activity: Design
  • Start date: Monday
  • Days of work: 2
  • Days over: 2

This creates two full days of work. The example shows values to enter; do not change your company's settings just to match it.

Why does my form look different?

Days is the default for a new company. An existing company may use Hours, which asks for hours per day, or Blocks, which records a date span without an amount and does not calculate utilisation. Check Settings to see the company's scheduling mode.

An All projects activity may be assigned to a project or left unattributed. The Project field also offers Internal for internal work. New bookings must start on an effective working day. Time off and closures remain separate; the form may warn about an overlap. See Find capacity first, or resolve an overbooked result.
