Schedule

For: Owner, Admin, Editor, Viewer

How do we get next week ready? ​

CapacityLens is installed and the Owner has created the company. Start with Schedule to see the week, add the people and work the team needs, then book and review next week. You can enter any page when you know the task; nobody needs to read all ten pages in order.

Who can do each part? ​

  • Owner or Admin: invite teammates and manage their company access. Either can prepare the plan and hand daily planning to an Editor.
  • Editor: add schedule people, clients, projects and activities; record time off; create or change bookings.
  • Viewer: read the plan and find their work. They ask the planner to make corrections.

A schedule person (a resource row) and a teammate who can sign in (a member) are separate records. Create the row before booking someone; invite them separately if they need access. Linking a member to a row is an Owner/Admin task.

Start where you need help ​

  • Invite a teammate or recover a missing invitation.
  • Add people, then prepare clients and work.
  • Record time off, then find capacity.
  • Book work, resolve overbooking, or check your week as a Viewer.
  • Update the plan safely during the next review.

Each page stands on its own. An Owner who has delegated scheduling can stop after setting up access; the Admin or Editor can continue from the relevant task.
