Team & access

For: Owner, Admin, invitee

How does a teammate get access? ​

Only an Owner or Admin can invite someone or manage membership. Open Team & access, choose Invite someone, select the access Role, enter the teammate's email address, then choose Create invite. The invitation is sent to that address; the acceptance link is not displayed in the app.

The teammate opens the email, enters their name and chooses a password, then selects Accept invitation. An invitation gives them the invited company role; it does not add a schedule person. See Add people to create and optionally link a resource row.

Why might approval still be needed? ​

Admission depends on the installation's settings. It may check an allowed email domain, invitation evidence and Owner/Admin approval. If approval is required, the invitee selects Ask for access after accepting. An Owner or Admin opens Team & access, finds Waiting for approval, and selects Approve. The invitee selects Check again after the decision. Until admitted, they cannot reach company planning data.

The invite is missing or does not work ​

Ask an Owner or Admin to check Outstanding invites in Team & access. If the invitation is listed, Resend emails a fresh link; the previous link stops working. Invitations expire after seven days. If no invitation is listed, confirm the intended email address and ask the Owner/Admin to create one. If the server cannot send email, an operator must restore mail delivery. Never copy an invitation link into a message or example.

An address that already has an account cannot accept a new-identity invitation. Ask the installation operator to record invitation evidence for that existing account.
