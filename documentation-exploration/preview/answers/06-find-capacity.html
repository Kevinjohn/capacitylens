Schedule

For: Owner, Admin, Editor, Viewer

How much room is there next week? ​

On Schedule, use Today to return to the current week and Prev or Next to move one week. Choose Weeks visible to show 1, 2, 4, 6 or 8 weeks. The utilisation percentage beside each person describes only the dates currently visible. Changing the visible span can therefore change the percentage.

The red warning percentage has a different range: it flags over-capacity work within the next 14 days, regardless of zoom or panning. Red cells mark individual over-capacity days. An empty cell alone does not prove that a person is available; check their working pattern, time off, closure dates and availability dates too.

Want a week-by-week comparison? ​

Open Overview for free capacity by week. By default, only Owners and Admins can open it. An Owner or Admin can change Overview access in Settings to Admins & Editors or Everyone. This setting controls access to the Overview page; it does not hide schedule data elsewhere.

Overview is optional. A company using Blocks has no capacity figures there, because Blocks do not measure hours. Do not conclude that the plan is free of overbooking just because an average is at or below 100%; inspect individual days and the red warning. Use Book work after checking the destination person's working pattern and dates.
