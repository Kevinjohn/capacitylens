Step 2 of 10 · Team & access

For: Owner or Admin

Give Bruce access ​

Diana wants Bruce to see the schedule, not change it. In Team & access, she selects Invite someone, chooses Viewer, enters Bruce’s email address, and selects Create invite. CapacityLens emails the invitation; it does not display a copyable link in the app.

Accepting the invitation creates a sign-in and membership with the invited role; admission may still be required. It does not create Bruce’s schedule row. Diana will add that separately in Add schedule people.

Bruce opens the invitation from his email, enters his name and a password, then selects Accept invitation. The company may also require admission approval, depending on its configuration. If Bruce sees Ask for access, he selects it. Diana opens Team & access → Waiting for approval, finds Bruce, and selects Approve. Bruce then selects Check again on the waiting screen to continue. If the company does not require this approval step, Bruce can continue once the access screen says he is admitted.

If the invitation is missing ​

Diana checks Outstanding invites in Team & access. If Bruce’s address is listed, she selects Resend to email a fresh invitation. A resent invitation replaces the earlier link. If there is no invitation to resend, Diana checks the address and tries Invite someone again.

Invitations need working email delivery on the server. If the invitation cannot be emailed, or Bruce cannot get past the access screen, ask the person responsible for the installation to check email delivery or the company’s admission rules. Do not ask Bruce to forward or paste an invitation link into chat.

An Editor or Viewer can open Team & access to see their own role, but only Diana or another Owner/Admin can send invitations or approve access. For what Bruce can do after he joins, continue with Check a week as Bruce.
