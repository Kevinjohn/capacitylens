Step 1 of 10 · Schedule

For: Owner or Admin

Plan next week ​

This walkthrough starts after CapacityLens is installed and an Owner has created the company. Diana is the Owner at Wayne Enterprises. She wants a clear plan for next week and wants Bruce to be able to see his work.

The example assumes the company works Monday through Friday, both schedule rows are full days, planning uses Days, and no work is booked yet. A new company starts in Days mode, but an existing company may use another mode. Keep its current setting; where the example uses Days, enter an equivalent amount in the mode you already use.

Follow the work that applies ​

  1. Diana invites Bruce as a Viewer. Bruce accepts and asks for access, and Diana approves him: Give Bruce access.
  2. Add Bruce and Diana to the schedule. Diana can optionally link Bruce’s sign-in to his schedule row; he can open its work list without that link: Add schedule people.
  3. Create Wayne Enterprises, its Website refresh project, and the project-specific Design activity: Set up the work.
  4. Record Bruce’s Friday off before entering work: Record the absence.
  5. Check the week and the team’s capacity: Find room in the week.
  6. Book four days for Bruce from Monday through Thursday: Book the first work.
  7. When one more day arrives for Bruce on Monday, reassign it to Diana and check the red day clears: Resolve the overbooking.
  8. Bruce checks his own work as a Viewer: Check a week as Bruce.

The pages are entry points, not a course. If the company already has people, work, or invitations, start at the page that fills the gap. For example, an Admin can take over day-to-day planning at Resources without needing Diana to work through every setup page first.

The outcome is a schedule the team can review and adjust each week. The plan lives on Schedule; CapacityLens does not send it to the team or publish it elsewhere.

When something changes, use the recovery guidance on Update the plan.
