Step 7 of 10 · Schedule and New allocation

For: Editor, Admin or Owner

Book the first work ​

Diana has checked Bruce’s working days and Friday time off. This example assumes the company works Monday through Friday, Bruce is available for full days, and Days is the company’s current planning mode. No work is booked on these dates yet.

  1. On Schedule, choose Work in the filter row if time-off drawing is selected, then select + on Bruce’s row to open New allocation for the visible week. Set the start date to Monday.
  2. Choose Website refresh in Project, then Design in Activity.
  3. Enter 4 in Days of work and 4 in Days over. The date span should end Thursday.
  4. Check the dates, then select Save.

The saved bar spans Monday through Thursday on Bruce’s row. Friday stays free for his recorded time off. Days of work describes an amount spread over the span; it is not a timesheet entry. Only an Editor, Admin or Owner can book work.

If your company uses Hours or Blocks, keep its existing mode and use the amount fields shown for that mode; don’t change company settings for this example. The Days fields above apply only in Days mode. Blocks do not measure utilisation.

If new work also arrives for Bruce on Monday, resolve the overbooking. CapacityLens marks work above available capacity for review; it does not move the booking automatically.
