Step 9 of 10 · Schedule

For: Viewer

Check a week as Bruce ​

Bruce has joined as a Viewer. His resource row exists on the schedule; a member link is not required to open its work list. He opens Schedule and selects the avatar beside his row. His work list includes bookings and personal time off for the current company week and the next three weeks. It stays on that fixed range when he changes Weeks visible, pans the grid, or filters it.

The list can still show Bruce’s work when a client, project, activity, person search or Tentative filter hides it from the grid. Each booking shows its activity, project and client, dates, and daily hours when the company’s mode measures hours. A task or note appears only when one exists. The work list leaves out company closures and ordinary non-working days.

If the row is hard to find, Bruce selects Show filters, searches for his schedule name, then checks the active filters. Clear Filters removes them, and Today returns the grid to the current week. The work list is the direct view of his booked work; it does not send a schedule or notify anyone.

Bruce can read the schedule but cannot edit bookings, resources, or time off. If Friday time off is missing, the wrong schedule row is linked, or the booking details need changing, he asks Diana or another person with editing access. An Owner sees confidential real names. Other roles, including Bruce’s Viewer role, see code names when the company keeps those names private.

If Bruce cannot find his row after clearing filters, Diana checks Resources to make sure it exists. Anyone can open a visible row’s work list from its avatar, even without a member link. An Owner or Admin manages Link to Resource in Team & access separately; inviting someone does not create a schedule row.
