Company login ​

Sign in with a password or Google, depending on what the installer has turned on. The demo, and an installation with CAPACITYLENS_MODE=off, have no sign-in.

With Google sign-in, CapacityLens sends you to Google and never sees your Google password. A Google account alone does not grant access: the first person on an empty installation needs the installer's approval, and later new Google identities need an addressed invitation. Existing company members can still join another company when its joining policy allows them.

Passwords and company sign-in ​

In mixed mode (password-and-sso), Sign in with Google appears above the password form with an or use your password divider. Google-only sign-in shows no password form; password-only sign-in shows no provider action.

On a fresh installation, the first Google identity must use an address in the operator's bootstrap allowlist. Signing in creates the identity, not a company; that Owner then completes company setup.

When Google sign-in is required (sso-only), password sessions cannot satisfy it. The company provider is Google.

Set up Google sign-in covers registration and server settings. Require Google sign-in covers the operator procedure.

Invitations and the first Owner ​

On an empty installation, the installer can allow a specific email address to create the first Google identity. That person follows Set up your company to become its Owner. The allowance closes once the first identity exists.

After that, the Owner chooses Who can join in Team & access: Invitation only (the default), open registration, approved email domains, or approved domains plus invitations. An Admin can see the policy and ask the Owner to change it. An existing Google identity with a verified address can join through a policy and receives Viewer access; an addressed invitation can grant its chosen role. Approved domains match exactly, so team.example.com is not example.com.

To join through a company link, open the page, sign in with an existing Google identity or password account, then select Join company. The server checks the current company policy and trusted email proof when the person completes the join:

  • A new Google identity cannot be created through open or domain registration. It needs an unused invitation addressed to its verified Google email.
  • Password signup through an open or domain policy is not available.
  • When email delivery is enabled, a password user can choose Email me a verification link and open it within 60 minutes while signed in to join through an open or approved-domain policy.
  • A Google callback never adds company membership or consumes an invitation. The person must explicitly accept an invitation or complete the company join.

A company can disable someone's access even when its joining policy allows them.

Connect an existing account ​

  1. Sign in to your existing account and open Account → Security.
  2. Under Company sign-in, choose Connect Google. You may be asked to confirm it's you.
  3. Sign in at Google with the same email address. Your local email must already be verified, and the Google address must match it.

Connecting keeps your memberships and work. CapacityLens never merges accounts because their email addresses match, and a Google identity already connected to someone else cannot be connected again. Google sign-in uses the provider's stable account identity; an email change does not connect a different Google account.

If connecting fails ​

  • You cancel Confirm it's you. No connection starts.
  • Confirmation cannot refresh your session. Sign out, sign in again and retry.
  • Your local email is unverified. When password sign-in and email verification are available, open the company's Join company link, sign in, and choose Join company. When prompted, choose Email me a verification link and open it within 60 minutes in the same signed-in session. The link returns you to the join page; choose Join company again to verify the address and finish joining when company policy allows it. Then retry Connect Google. Google must return that same verified address. If email verification is unavailable, ask your installer about the company's supported sign-in options.

Sessions and sensitive actions ​

A session lasts at most twelve hours from sign-in; activity does not extend it. Thirty minutes without activity also signs you out.

These actions ask you to confirm your identity again when needed: connecting or repairing a company identity, transferring ownership, resetting another member's password, revoking another member's sessions, deleting a company, and importing or purging company data. Invitations, role changes and removing members need only the right role.

If confirming through Google takes you away from the page, return and submit the action again. Confirming does not apply the original change.

Multi-factor sign-in at Google ​

CapacityLens has no local MFA. If your agency requires MFA, set it up at Google. Enabling company sign-in does not turn it on, and CapacityLens cannot see or enforce Google's policy or tell whether a challenge happened. Test the policy, its recovery path and session behaviour before relying on it.

When someone leaves ​

  1. Disable their work account at the provider.
  2. In Team & access, remove their membership or select Disable Access.
  3. Select Revoke sessions to end their existing CapacityLens sessions across all their companies. This needs the corresponding authority. Disabling them at the provider does not end those sessions.

If someone returns with a new sign-in ​

A Disable Access restriction follows a new sign-in with the same address only when both identities proved mailbox ownership, through verified Google sign-in or a completed mailbox check. An invitation or an older "email verified" flag does not count. An established Google sign-in keeps its stable identity. A returning Google sign-in refreshes mailbox proof only when its verified address exactly matches the local account email; it never changes that email or merges accounts. An Owner or Admin can still manage the original restriction from No longer active in Team & access.
