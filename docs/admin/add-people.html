Add people to the schedule ​

This task adds the people whose capacity your company plans. A scheduled person does not need a sign-in.

Steps ​

  1. Open Resources and select Add resource. Choose Person for a named person. Use a placeholder only for an unfilled role attached to a project.
  2. Enter the person's details. Add their name, role, engagement, and discipline when disciplines are enabled.
  3. Set Working days. Mark each weekday as Full day, Half day, or Not working. Add start and end dates when their availability is time-limited.
  4. Save the person. They appear in Resources and as a row on Schedule.
  5. Link their sign-in when appropriate. Open Team & access, find the member, and use the Scheduled person control. A linked member can be recognised consistently, but linking does not change their role.

What's next ​

Choose company settings, or read People and placeholders for engagement types, placeholders, and member links.
