Invite teammates ​

Invite people, choose what they can do, and later change or stop their access. Only an Owner or Admin can do this. An Editor or Viewer who opens Team & access sees their own role instead of Invite someone.

Send an invitation ​

  1. Open Team & access and select Invite someone.
  2. Choose the Role. The dialog spells out what the role can and can't do, and See full capabilities lists everything. Roles and permissions compares the roles.
  3. Enter the teammate's email. Only that address can accept the invitation.
  4. Select Create invite. CapacityLens emails the link.

Invitations need the server's email delivery. Without it, the dialog says so and nothing is created; ask your installer to set up email. If the invitation was saved but the email failed, a warning says so: select Resend on its row to try again.

The link is never shown in CapacityLens. It works once and expires after seven days. Resend emails a fresh link and stops the old one working. An invitation never adds the person to the schedule: link their membership to a Resource after they join.

Check what the invitee sees ​

The link shows your company name and the invited role. The invitee enters their name, chooses a password and selects Accept invitation. An invitation creates a new sign-in, so an address that already has one is refused; the installer can record the invitation for that account instead. Sign-in and access has their steps.

Approve new people ​

After accepting, the person selects Ask for access and appears under Waiting for approval in Team & access, with the role their invitation offered. Select Approve to let them in with that role, or Decline to refuse. They see the result the next time they select Check again. Nobody can reach company data before an Owner or Admin approves them.

A declined person stays in the list, marked Declined, so you can still select Approve if you declined by mistake.

Revoke an invitation ​

Invitations not yet accepted are listed under Outstanding invites, above your members. Select Revoke to cancel an invitation that has not expired, or Resend to email a fresh link.

Change a member's role or schedule row ​

Send new members See your work. Members are listed by role, Owner first. On each row:

  • The edit icon changes their role. The dialog explains the consequences before you Save role.
  • The link icon opens Link to Resource. Choosing a person saves immediately and does not change their access. Remove link to resource unlinks them immediately. Link a person to a member
  • More actions offers the rest: Disable Access, Archive user or Remove.

You cannot reset another member's password or end their sessions from CapacityLens. A member who is locked out selects Forgot password? on the sign-in page; the installer can help otherwise.

Stop access ​

All three are in More actions. None affects the person's other company memberships.

  • Use Disable Access when they must not get back in. It works at once, including on an existing session or a later invitation, and stays in force even after removal until an Owner or Admin selects Enable Access (also offered under No longer active).
  • Use Archive user when they leave but you want to keep their membership on record. It stops their access; Restore membership reactivates it but doesn't clear Disable Access.
  • Use Remove when they no longer belong to the company. It ends that membership and keeps them out: they move to No longer active, and Enable Access brings them back with the role they held when removed.

Invitations go only to addresses without an account, so a person who has signed in before comes back through Restore membership or Enable Access, never a new invitation. After Enable Access, an active member keeps their role and an archived person still needs restoring.

A disabled or removed person cannot rejoin until an Owner or Admin selects Enable Access under No longer active.

A new installation allows one company. Ask your installer if you need more; Configuration has the setting.
