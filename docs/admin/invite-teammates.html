Invite teammates ​

Invite people, choose what they can do, and later change or stop their access. Only an Owner or Admin can do this. An Editor or Viewer who opens Team & access sees their own role instead of Invite someone.

Send an invitation ​

  1. Open Team & access and select Invite someone.
  2. Choose the Role. The dialog spells out what the role can and can't do, and See full capabilities lists everything. Roles and permissions compares the roles.
  3. Enter the teammate's email. Only that address can accept the invitation.
  4. If the person already has a row on the schedule, choose it under Link to Resource. Otherwise leave No Resource linked.
  5. Select Create invite. If email is set up, CapacityLens sends the invitation. If it isn't, or sending fails, select Copy and send the link privately.

The link is shown only in this dialog. CapacityLens keeps only a hash of it, so nobody can retrieve it later. If it is lost, revoke the invitation under Outstanding invites and create a new one; the old link stops working at once. If you close the dialog before the invitation is created, a notice says the link was not shown.

If the linked person is no longer available ​

The Link to Resource choice is a proposal: it does not reserve the person, and the recipient cannot see it. If that person is no longer available when the invitation is accepted, the member still joins and their row shows Resource link needs attention. Select Choose another person or Dismiss.

Check what the invitee sees ​

The link opens a preview of your company name, the proposed role, what it allows and when the link expires. Opening it does not change membership. They then:

  • sign in with their existing password and select Accept invite;
  • choose Create account, which creates the sign-in and accepts in one step; or
  • choose a company login provider, return to the invitation and select Accept invite.

The preview shows only the start of the invited address, such as selina.kyle@…. They must enter the full address. Entering a different one cannot change who the invitation is for. When they accept, they land on your schedule.

Revoke an invitation ​

Invitations not yet accepted are listed under Outstanding invites, above your members. A proposed Resource stays marked pending until the invitation is accepted. Select Revoke to cancel an invitation.

Change a member's role or schedule row ​

Send new members See your work. Members are listed by role, Owner first. On each row:

  • The edit icon changes their role. The dialog explains the consequences before you Save role.
  • The link icon opens Link to Resource. Choosing a person saves immediately and does not change their access. Remove link to resource unlinks them immediately. Link a person to a member
  • More actions offers the rest: reset their password, Revoke sessions, Disable Access, Archive user or Remove.

Stop access ​

All three are in More actions. None affects the person's other company memberships.

  • Disable Access stops them opening the company immediately, including through an existing session or a later invitation. It stays in force even after removal, until an Owner or Admin selects Enable Access, which is also available for people under No longer active.
  • Archive user stops their access but keeps an inactive record. Restore membership reactivates it, but does not clear Disable Access.
  • Remove ends the membership. They may return later if the joining policy allows it.

Archiving or removing does not block a new invitation; a returning person gets the invitation's role. After Enable Access, an active member keeps their role, but an archived or removed person still needs restoring or a new invitation.

If they return with a new sign-in, check No longer active: How sign-in works explains when the restriction follows them.

Review who can join ​

Team & access → Who can join shows the joining policy and approved domains. Only the Owner can change it; ask them if a teammate cannot join or registration should be tighter. Copy the Joining link to share it; the policy still decides who gets in. People who join through the policy get Viewer, so send an addressed invitation for any other role. Decide who can join explains each policy.

A fresh install allows one company. The installer can allow more with CAPACITYLENS_MULTI_ACCOUNT (Configuration).
