Verify and hand over ​

Before people use the installation, confirm that the service is stable, HTTPS reaches the application, and its data survives a restart.

Check the running service ​

From the host, check liveness and readiness through the public HTTPS address:

bash
curl -fsS https://capacity.example.com/health/live
curl -fsS https://capacity.example.com/health/ready

Liveness only proves that the process answers. Readiness checks the database and its schema integrity. Investigate a failing response before handover; verify backups separately.

Confirm persistence and backups ​

Sign in with the setup token, create the first Owner and company, and make a harmless test change. Restart the service, sign in again, and confirm the change remains. Check that a completed snapshot exists under CAPACITYLENS_BACKUP_DIR and copy a protected snapshot off-host. Follow Backups and restore to validate recovery on an isolated path.

Hand over access ​

Give the Owner the public address, the setup instructions and the relevant operator contacts. Send any setup token separately, then remove it from the environment and restart once the first identity exists. Agree who owns upgrades, backups, readiness monitoring and future sign-in configuration. Do not send secrets through deployment logs or public support channels.
