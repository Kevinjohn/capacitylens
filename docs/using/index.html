See your work ​

Someone at your agency has invited you. CapacityLens shows what you're booked on, when, and who else is busy. You don't need to install or set up anything.

  1. Accept the invitation. Open the link from your email. Sign in, or select Create account if you're new, using the address the invitation was sent to. Then select Accept invite. Join your team

  2. Find your week. You land on Schedule. Find your name and select the avatar beside it. Your bookings, time off and remaining capacity open in one list.

  3. Read the schedule. Each bar is a booking: its project, its dates and how much of each day it takes. Red means someone has more work than time that day. The percentage beside a name shows how full they are across the weeks on screen. Read the schedule

  4. Look further ahead. Use Weeks visible to show up to eight weeks, and Today, Prev and Next to move. Show filters narrows the view to one client, project or activity.

That's all most people need. If something looks wrong, tell whoever books the work: with the Viewer role you can look but not change.

If you book the work ​

With the Editor role you can also change the plan. Drag across empty days to book someone, drag a bar to move it, and select Undo if you change your mind. Plan work as an Editor covers the rest.

Pages ​

  • Schedule: the day-by-day grid of bookings, utilisation and time off.
  • Overview: each person's free days, week by week. Owners and Admins only, unless your company opens it to everyone.
  • Resources: the people, placeholders and partners you can schedule.
  • Disciplines: the coloured groups for people and schedule rows.
  • Clients and projects: the organisations and projects behind each booking.
  • Activities: the kinds of work you book.
  • Time off: holidays, absences and company closures.
  • Team & access: your role and, for Owners and Admins, the team's access.
  • Settings: company planning rules and your own display preferences.
  • Help: a short tour for your role.
  • Account: your sign-in and security.
