Read the schedule ​

This page helps every role understand the main Schedule without changing anything.

Steps ​

  1. Open Schedule. Each row represents a person or placeholder. Rows can be grouped by discipline or engagement type.
  2. Read the dates from left to right. Use Weeks visible to choose the amount of time shown, and Today to return to the current week.
  3. Read each bar. A work bar names its activity and covers the days allocated to it. Time-off bars identify absence. Company closure bands apply across the schedule.
  4. Check utilisation. The percentage beside a person compares their allocated time with their available time in the visible period. Over-capacity means allocated time is greater than available time.
  5. Open a booking for detail. Select a bar to see its dates, amount, activity, project, and status. A Viewer can inspect it without receiving edit controls.
