Find available capacity ​

This page helps every role find who has space in the weeks that matter.

Steps ​

  1. Choose the period on Schedule. Use Weeks visible and move to the dates you need.
  2. Open the filters. Narrow the view by person, discipline, client, project, or activity when the full schedule is too broad.
  3. Look for open working days. Greyed non-working days do not count as capacity. Check time off and company closures before treating an empty cell as available.
  4. Compare utilisation. The visible percentage uses the dates currently on screen, so change the window before comparing people.
  5. Use Overview when available. Overview gives a compact weekly comparison. Some companies limit it to Owners and Admins; its absence does not mean your sign-in is broken.

What's next ​

If your role permits changes, schedule work. See the Overview reference for its display modes and access setting.
