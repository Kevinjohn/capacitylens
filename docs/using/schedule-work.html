Schedule and change work ​

An allocation books a person's time against an activity for a date range. It appears as a bar on the Schedule. Editors, Admins and Owners can add and change allocations.

Schedule work ​

You need the person in Resources and an activity to book. Clients and projects and Activities cover setting those up.

Open Schedule and select the + button on the person's row. The form opens with the visible week filled in. Or drag across empty days on their row to choose the dates; clicking one empty cell opens the form for that day.

Choose Project, then Activity. Check the dates, amount and status, then select Save. The booking appears on the person's row.

Change or remove scheduled work ​

Move or reassign a booking ​

Drag the middle of the bar along the row to move it, or onto another person's row to reassign it. The booking keeps its number of working days, so a different working pattern can change the calendar dates it spans.

Change the date span ​

Drag the left or right edge of the bar.

Edit details or delete the booking ​

Click the bar to open Edit allocation. Change the assignee, project, activity, dates, amount, status, task or note, then select Save. Select Delete to remove it.

Select Undo in the toolbar, or press Ctrl/Cmd+Z, if you change or remove the wrong booking.

Create an allocation ​

Project and activity ​

Choose Internal, No specific project or a real project:

  • Internal offers only internal activities.
  • No specific project offers only All-projects activities and leaves the booking unattributed.
  • A real project offers the shared All projects activities first, then that project's Project-specific ones.

An All-projects activity booked under an active project, with an active client, counts towards that project. The bar takes the project's label, colour and filters. Choosing No specific project when editing removes that attribution. Existing unattributed bookings stay unattributed until you change them.

If the activity doesn't exist, create it on Activities. If your company has turned on inline Add activity in Settings, you can add it from the form; under a real project it becomes project-specific.

Status, task and note ​

Status is Confirmed, Tentative or Completed. Notes are one line. When Show task field in schedule is on in Settings, the form also has an optional one-line Task, kept when the setting is turned off.

Amount ​

In Hours mode, Hours / day offers 1 hour, Quarter day (2h), Half day (4h) or Full day (8h). An existing booking with another value keeps showing that exact value until you choose an option.

In Days mode, Days of work can be a positive fraction; the half-day steps are only suggestions. The amount spread across Days over cannot exceed 24 hours per day.

Working days ​

Leave Ignore working days unticked to book only the days in both the company-wide working days and the person's own pattern. Tick it to use every calendar day in the span, including non-working weekdays.

Either way, a new allocation must start on a working day that isn't covered by the person's time off. To book across closed days, start on an open day with the box ticked, then drag or extend the bar. A booking must also stay within the person's availability dates, and Ignore working days does not bypass them. A booking that falls outside those dates later stays visible, and you can still edit its details; only changes to its dates or assignee are blocked.

External-party bookings always use literal calendar days, so they have no checkbox.

Repeat ​

Under Repeat, choose Weekly, Every 2 weeks, Every 3 weeks, Every 4 weeks or Monthly, then set Repeat until. The date cannot be before today or the start, must allow at least one repeat, and can be at most six calendar months after the start. An occurrence that starts on or before it may finish after it. The form shows how many bookings it will create and the last start date. Leave Doesn’t repeat for a single booking.

Saving creates the whole series, and one Undo removes it. After that, each occurrence is edited on its own. Deleting one lets you remove just that occurrence or it and every later one. With an All-projects activity, every occurrence keeps the chosen project.

Edit, move and remove allocations ​

  • Moving to another row keeps the start date. The drop is refused, and the bar stays put, if that date is outside the company's or person's working pattern or the person's availability dates. With Ignore working days on, the bar may use non-working days but still not cross availability dates.
  • Reassigning keeps the number of working days, not the dates. Two days of work running Thursday to the following Tuesday, for someone who works neither Fridays nor Mondays, becomes Thursday and Friday for a Monday-to-Friday colleague. While you drag, the bar shows the length you will get, and does not move if the drop will be refused.
  • Resizing outside the person's availability dates is refused, leaving the booking unchanged.
  • Opening a bar lets you change its status, duplicate it or delete it. Repeat occurrences cannot be duplicated.

Archiving a project hides its bookings until you restore it. Permanently deleting a project removes its project-specific bookings; shared All-projects bookings remain, unattributed.

How allocation granularity works ​

An allocation is a date range plus how much of each day it takes. How you enter that depends on your company's scheduling mode in Settings:

  • Hours: start date, end date and hours per day.
  • Days, the default for a new company: how many days of work, spread over how many days. CapacityLens works out the hours per day.
  • Blocks: a span of days with no hours, which doesn't count toward utilisation.

Changing the mode does not rewrite existing allocations. CapacityLens plans capacity; it is not a timesheet. What is CapacityLens? explains why.

Next: record absences in Time off. The form warns you when a booking overlaps one.
