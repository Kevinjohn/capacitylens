Owner FAQ ​

Can I stop after I appoint an Admin? ​

Yes. Once their row in Team & access shows Admin, send them Get the schedule ready. If you run the schedule yourself, follow that guide instead.

Why is the Admin invitation still pending? ​

They have not accepted it yet. Check the email was sent, or send the link yourself with Copy. If the link is lost, revoke it under Outstanding invites and create a new one.

Does an Admin invitation transfer ownership? ​

No. An invitation gives someone the role in the invitation, but cannot transfer ownership. Only the current Owner can choose an active, unrestricted Admin in Manage ownership and confirm the transfer. You become Admin and the selected Admin becomes Owner immediately, without a separate consent step. There is only ever one Owner; give other managers the Admin role. Transfer ownership

Why is there no Admin to choose as Next Owner? ​

Only an active, unrestricted Admin can be selected. Invite them as Admin first, then return once they accept.

What happens after I confirm the transfer? ​

The selected Admin becomes Owner and you become Admin immediately. There is no pending request for them to accept.

Can I change the week start, time zone or language? ​

No. Week start and time zone are fixed when the company is created, and English is the only language.

Who can change the joining policy? ​

Only you. Admins can see it and copy the joining link. People who join through the policy get Viewer; send an addressed invitation for any other role. Decide who can join

Why can't I find Delete company? ​

The app does not provide a company chooser or deletion action. A standard installation holds one company: export a copy, then ask the installer to retire the installation and its data. Delete the company

Why am I asked to confirm my sign-in? ​

Transferring ownership, importing and deleting the company need a recent sign-in.

What does it cost? ​

Nothing. CapacityLens is free and open source (AGPL-3.0), with no paid tier and no hosted version. The only cost is the server it runs on (server size).

Is our client information private? ​

Your data stays on your own server. Your installer, who runs that server, can reach it. To hide a sensitive client or project name from everyone except you, give it a code name. Privacy covers what is stored and where.

How do I see who's overloaded? ​

On Schedule, a day shows red when someone is booked for more than they can do. Overview shows each person's free capacity week by week.

How do we get our data out? ​

Open Settings → Data and support → Import and export and select Export JSON. The file holds the company's scheduling records, such as people, clients, projects, bookings and time off, and can be imported into another CapacityLens installation. It does not include members or settings. There is no CSV or spreadsheet export.

My Admin is leaving. What do I do? ​

Invite the new Admin first. Then, in Team & access, open the departing Admin's More actions and select Disable Access: they can no longer open the company, and their record stays. Their row on the schedule and their bookings are not affected. Stop access explains the other options.

What if I leave? ​

Hand the company to an Admin with a transfer of ownership.
