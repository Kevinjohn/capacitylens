Understand Owner responsibilities ​

This page explains the small set of decisions that remain with the Owner after an Admin takes over everyday setup.

What stays with the Owner ​

  • There is exactly one active Owner for a company.
  • Only the Owner can start and complete a transfer of ownership.
  • Only the Owner can delete the company.
  • Only the Owner can turn member sign-in recording on or off.
  • Some installation-wide problems still need the person who operates the server.

Sensitive actions may ask you to confirm your sign-in again. This protects changes with large consequences even when your current session is still open.

Work your Admin can own ​

An Admin can invite and manage other members, choose their roles, add people and work, change most company settings, and keep the schedule ready for the team. The Admin and settings guide covers that workflow from beginning to end.

What's next ​

See Roles and permissions for the full role comparison, or Owner FAQ for common handover questions.
